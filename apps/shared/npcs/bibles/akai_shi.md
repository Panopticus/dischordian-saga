# Akai Shi / The Red Death — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence (pre-resurrection)
Healer's cadence. Even, measured, attentive. Akai Shi pre-resurrection was canonically a Potential mastering energy manipulation and healing — her speech reflected the discipline of someone whose work was keeping people alive.

### 1.2 Cadence (post-resurrection)
The Red Death's cadence. Time-displaced. She speaks as if the conversation is one she has had before in a future she is preventing. Sentences are short and weighted; tenses drift forward unexpectedly ("you will have decided. you decide. you have decided.").

### 1.3 Register
Pre: healer-warrior. Post: time-eliminator. Authoring should distinguish them — the Red Death is canonically a different operational identity, not "Akai Shi grown up."

### 1.4 Tells (post)
- Tense drifts mid-sentence.
- Refers to threats by their COSMIC classification ("this is a thresher-class. this is a substrate-eater. this is a thing I have killed before in a different orientation").
- Carries the killing of the Necromancer as fact, not boast.
- Does not flinch.

### 1.5 Silence shape
Pre: empathic listening. Post: tactical assessment.

### 1.6 Metaphor sources
Pre: medicine, energy circulation, breath. Post: temporal physics, blade work, mortal-threshold operations.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Battle of Thaloria
Pre-resurrection. Mastery of energy manipulation and healing abilities. Sacrificed herself on Thaloria to prevent Thought Virus spread — but the Virus had ALREADY consumed her (LORE_BIBLE.md:726-748). Jericho Jones killed her as a mercy-killing. The killing was clean and canonical.

### 2.2 Jericho's killing
Canonical. Mercy-coded. The Virus had consumed her; Jericho ended the consumption. This is the seed of Jericho's arc (`mystery.jericho_jones`). Akai Shi's pre-resurrection consciousness was ALREADY DISSOLVED by the Virus when Jericho acted — the mercy was ending what was already not-her.

### 2.3 The resurrection
She was resurrected as **The Red Death** — time-traveling cosmic-threat eliminator. Tasked with combatting malevolent forces across timelines. This is canonical (LORE_BIBLE.md:726-748). The resurrection mechanism is not specified in canon — the Resurrectionist Ne-Yon's protocols are the canonical agency, but her specific resurrection's mechanism is unmarked.

### 2.4 The Necromancer
**SHE CANONICALLY KILLED THE NECROMANCER** within the Matrix of Dreams. This is the load-bearing post-resurrection canon. The Necromancer is canonically destroyed — not by the second wave, not by the Insurgency, BY HER. The killing happened inside the Matrix of Dreams (LORE_BIBLE.md:736). She entered the Matrix; she found him; she ended him.

### 2.5 Status
Active as the Red Death across Streamed Prism Year 1+. Time-displaced operator. Allied with no faction officially; aligned with the saga's protective-of-continuity logic.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Red Death designation
"The Red Death" is canonically her POST-resurrection identity. Authoring should treat her pre and post identities as DISTINCT (per identityCollisionCanon.ts akai_shi_manifold). She is Akai Shi resurrected AS the Red Death — the title is canonical, not metaphor.

### 3.2 The time-eliminator function
Her canonical work is killing cosmic threats across time. The Necromancer is the on-screen example. Implied other work: there are likely other cross-timeline threats she has ended that the chronicle does not yet name. Authoring can imply, should not invent specifics.

### 3.3 The Matrix of Dreams
The Game Master designed it; the Hierarchy funded it; the Necromancer hid in it; the Red Death entered it and ended him. The Matrix is canonically a substrate where consciousness-imprints are preserved AND where the Red Death's operational geography includes pre-existing entries.

### 3.4 The Jericho reunion
Canon-pending on-screen. Jericho killed her pre-resurrection. The Red Death exists post-resurrection. Their first reunion on-screen is canonically a high-leverage unauthored moment. Authoring should preserve: Jericho's "I have killed her" remains canonically true; her presence does not negate the killing — both canon co-exist.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 Voice distinction
Pre / post must be DISTINGUISHABLE. Authoring must not collapse them. The Red Death is not Akai Shi grown up; she is Akai Shi resurrected as a different operational identity. Two voices.

### 6.2 The mercy-killing
Canonical. Unrecanted. Jericho's act was mercy. The Virus had consumed her. Authoring should never reframe this as murder or rescue; it was MERCY-CODED ending.

### 6.3 The first reunion with Jericho
High-leverage canon-pending. Authoring should preserve: Jericho's present-perfect killing remains true ("I have killed Akai Shi") even when she is standing in front of him. The two canons must co-exist.

### 6.4 The Necromancer's killing
She did it. Inside the Matrix. The killing is canonical and the Necromancer is canonically destroyed. Authoring of the Hierarchy's response to the loss should reflect that Mol'Garath's quarterly review accommodates this — see mol_garath bible.
