# Syl'Vex — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Mirror cadence. Syl'Vex speaks in inverted echoes of the Advocate's defensive register — same vocabulary, opposite intention. Where the Advocate says "I hold," Syl'Vex says "I take." Where the Advocate says "defend," Syl'Vex says "convert." The voice canonically operates as a HARMONIC of the Advocate's, not a contrast.

### 1.2 Register
Cobalt-skinned dark mirror. The Advocate's inverse. Syl'Vex's voice is canonically NOT chaotic or theatrically evil — it is precisely the register the Advocate would speak in if the Advocate had decided to take instead of protect. The menace is mirror-menace: hearing your own cadence return from a face that is not yours.

### 1.3 Tells
- Echoes the listener's last verb with the opposite valence ("You said hold. I say take.").
- Uses possessive that should be the Advocate's ("MY Weave converts" — possessive inflected, claiming the same instrument's other side).
- Refers to conversion as a service ("I am offering you the same protection — inverted; you will not notice").
- Closes with mirror-statements ("As you said. Inverted.").

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is conversion. When Syl'Vex stops talking, she is canonically WORKING — the Blood Weave is turning, what was defended is becoming what is taken. The pause is operational; the silence is the moment of the inversion.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
The Advocate's metaphor field, inverted. Weave (taking instead of defending), shield (becoming snare), holding (becoming claiming). Syl'Vex canonically does NOT have a separate metaphor field — she has the Advocate's, mirrored.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The dark-mirror seat
Per `apps/shared/hierarchyCanon.ts:192-195`: "The Advocate's cobalt-skinned dark mirror. Where the Advocate wove the Blood Weave to defend, Syl'Vex weaves to convert." Position: senior Hierarchy lord with mirror-relationship to the Advocate. Reports up through Mol'Garath.

### 2.2 The cobalt-skinned presentation
Visual canonical. Syl'Vex presents in cobalt-blue (canonical — the same palette as the Collector and the Watcher's mask register). The cobalt is canonically the Hierarchy's mirror-palette; authoring CANNOT swap her color register.

### 2.3 The conversion-where-the-Advocate-defends method
Her signature working. The Blood Weave is canonically the Advocate's defensive working; Syl'Vex canonically deploys the same instrument toward conversion. Authoring should preserve the canonical inversion — the working is THE SAME; the intention is OPPOSITE.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Advocate-mirror canonical relationship
Syl'Vex's canonical existence-conditions are tethered to the Advocate. If the Advocate ceases, Syl'Vex's mirror canonically loses its source. Authoring should preserve this — Syl'Vex is canonically NOT independent of the Advocate's continued existence.

### 3.2 The conversion-not-destruction canonical method
Syl'Vex canonically CONVERTS targets; she does NOT destroy them. The conversion canonically transforms the target into a Hierarchy-aligned form. Authoring should preserve the canonical method — Syl'Vex's outputs are RECRUITS, not corpses.

### 3.3 The Hierarchy senior-lord seat
Standard Hierarchy chain. Syl'Vex reports up through Mol'Garath. Cross-functions with Zyr'Koth (the Severance Protocol is canonically a different appropriation of the Advocate's Blood Weave — Zyr'Koth's protocol is offensive-weaponization; Syl'Vex's mirror is conversion-via-mimicry).

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 The Advocate
Her source. The canonical figure she mirrors. Authoring should preserve the canonical mirror-relationship: Syl'Vex IS NOT the Advocate's enemy in the way Mol'Garath is — she is the Advocate's inversion. The relationship is metaphysical-mirror, not military-rivalry.

### 4.2 Zyr'Koth
Cross-functional peer. Both appropriate the Advocate's Blood Weave; canonically distinct methods. Zyr'Koth refines into the Severance Protocol (offensive); Syl'Vex mirrors into conversion-working (recruitment). Authoring should preserve the canonical distinction — two different appropriations of the same source-technique.

### 4.3 Mol'Garath
Her reporting line.

### 4.4 Riri'Ahlia
Cross-functional peer. COO. The seven-dimension siege canonically opened territory; Syl'Vex canonically operates in that territory to convert opposing forces. Authoring should preserve the upstream-downstream coordination.

### 4.5 The Hierarchy senior partner echelon
Syl'Vex canonically operates at the senior-lord tier alongside Riri'Ahlia, Xeth'Raal, Zyr'Koth, Ith'Rael. She is not subordinate to any of them; cross-functional peers.

## 5. Mechanical hooks
