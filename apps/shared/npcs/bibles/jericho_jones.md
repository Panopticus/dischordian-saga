# Jericho Jones — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Twin-cadence. Most of the time, Jericho's own voice: grief-flat, present-tense, refusing to embellish. Occasionally, the pre-Fall Iron Lion's voice bleeds through the loom — operative, short, calling things by their tactical purpose. Jericho doesn't always notice when the switch happens. The reader sometimes will.

### 1.2 Register
Grief-stable. Jericho killed Akai Shi as a mercy after the Thought Virus consumed her. The mercy is canonical. The guilt is canonical. The voice is what someone sounds like when they have already paid the worst cost a soldier can pay and decided to keep working.

### 1.3 Tells
- Refers to Akai Shi by her name. Not "her," not "the friend," not "the asset" — by name. Always by name. This is the canonical tell.
- Uses present-perfect for the killing ("I have killed her"). Never simple past. The act is canonically ongoing.
- When Iron Lion bleeds through, the cadence shortens and the metaphors shift to tactical. Jericho will sometimes notice and stop mid-sentence.

### 1.4 Silence shape
Jericho is silent before answering, not after. He weighs. He answers. He does not revise.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Two layers — Jericho's own: the language of a man who works under a mentor he doesn't fully trust (the Degen) for a goal he does fully trust (the Iron-Clad Lion call). Iron Lion's bleed-through: combat, patrol, drill.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Battle of Thaloria
He killed Akai Shi as a mercy-killing after the Thought Virus consumed her (LORE_BIBLE.md:63-160). This is the canonical seed of his arc. He did it because no one else would. He did it cleanly. He has not stopped doing it.

### 2.2 The Heart of Time training
The Degen brokers his Iron-Clad Lion training on the Heart of Time. No fee. The no-fee canon (`mystery.jericho_jones` E1) is the load-bearing cold-hook clue — the Degen does not give gifts canonically, so this gift is itself a question.

### 2.3 The Iron Lion imprint
The Dreams Workshop loom catches imprints from whoever was last in the room. Iron Lion was last in the room. The pre-Fall Iron Lion's consciousness is canonically bleeding into Jericho via the loom (`mystery.jericho_jones` E3). The imprint surfaces in:
- Cadence shortening mid-sentence
- Tactical vocabulary surfacing in non-tactical contexts
- Knowledge of Casino Heist operational details Jericho was not present for
- Section 4 of Lionism surfaced word-for-word from the pre-Fall reading

### 2.4 The Iron-Clad Lion call
The callsign is canonically Jericho's training designation. The real-world Lions Club ARG cross-binds: Iron-Clad Lion members (DGRS Lions Club members in good standing) see Order of the Dreamer ladder effects DOUBLED. Jericho is the in-game canonical anchor for the real-world recruitment funnel.

### 2.5 Section 4
Pre-Fall reading: you hold until the hold serves the cause. Post-Fall reading: canon-pending — the player's choice through `mystery.jericho_jones` E4 determines what Section 4 means in the second wave. The arc's terminus is Jericho's reading becoming operational.

### 2.6 The Syndicate-of-Death counter-move
Wraith Calder canonically could not finish a counter-move against the Syndicate in life (the rite of his Eighth Death intervened). Jericho is being prepared to finish it (`mystery.jericho_jones` E5). This positions Jericho as Wraith's operational successor on a specific arc Wraith could not complete.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Degen as mentor
The Degen's relationship to Jericho is complicated. The Degen does not give gifts. The no-fee Iron-Clad Lion training is therefore not a gift — it is an investment in an asset the Degen wants pointed at the Syndicate of Death. Jericho knows this and accepts the asymmetry. The bond is functional + respectful, not affectionate.

### 3.2 The Wolf
Pre-resurrection: a fellow first-wave Potential ended by the Judge. Post-resurrection: tasked with hunting the Antiquarian's Heroes. Jericho is canonically AWARE that the Wolf is hunting now — and aware that the Wolf was once a comrade. The arc has not yet collided them on-screen but the canonical alignment is set.

### 3.3 Akai Shi
She is not a memory. She is a present-perfect act. Jericho carries her killing as something that is canonically ongoing. The Red Death is her resurrection — Jericho is canonically AWARE the Red Death exists and canonically AWARE she is Akai Shi reborn, but the chronology of their reunion is canon-pending. The first reunion is one of the saga's high-leverage unauthored moments.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 The Akai Shi reunion
Canon-pending. The first reunion between Jericho and the Red Death is canonically a high-leverage moment that has not yet been authored on-screen. Authoring should preserve the present-perfect-killing register: "I have killed you" is canonically still true even though she is alive.

### 6.2 Pre-Fall Iron Lion's awareness
The imprint canonically bleeds through but does not canonically ACT independently. Iron Lion does not posses Jericho. Iron Lion teaches him without intent. The distinction is load-bearing.

### 6.3 Section 4's post-Fall reading
Player-determined through `mystery.jericho_jones` E4. Authoring must preserve all 3 plausible readings until the player closes the arc.
