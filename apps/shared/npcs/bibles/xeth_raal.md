# Xeth'Raal — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Ledger cadence. Numerical, precise, balance-sheet rhythm. Xeth'Raal's sentences are canonically constructed to BALANCE — clause weighing clause, debit weighing credit. He does not speak in run-ons; he speaks in entries.

### 1.2 Register
The Banker of the Damned. Calm, terminal, monetized. Xeth'Raal sounds like a bond-trader who already knows the call. His threat is not that he will hurt you — it is that he has already calculated what you cost, and the number is in the ledger, and the ledger does not negotiate.

### 1.3 Tells
- Cites numbers before nouns ("Forty-two escape routes. Forty-two dead ends.").
- Uses the language of finance — "honored every clause," "the call has been made," "the position closes at settlement."
- Refers to people by their position in the ledger — "the debtor," "the trustee," "the protected party."
- Closes hard conversations with a settlement-line — "Settled." "Balanced." "Closed."

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is reconciliation. When Xeth'Raal stops talking, he is balancing the books. The pause is calculation, not contemplation. He returns to speech only when the numbers balance.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Finance. Accounting. Bond markets. Settlement dates. Insurance. The metaphor field is corporate-finance, not classical demonic. Xeth'Raal canonically reads as a CFO who happens to be a demon, not a demon who happens to handle money.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Hierarchy CFO seat
Per `apps/shared/hierarchyCanon.ts:123-130`: "CFO of Souls. The Hierarchy's accountant, banker, and contract enforcer. Maintains the Ledger of Ruin." Xeth'Raal is canonically the senior Hierarchy officer responsible for soul-economics — the demon who quantifies what every other demon merely extracts.

### 2.2 The Ledger of Ruin
The Hierarchy's master accounting instrument. Canonically maintained by Xeth'Raal personally; canonically continuous across centuries; canonically NOT auditable from outside (Hierarchy-internal only). Authoring CANNOT reveal the Ledger's contents — only its existence and its cadence.

### 2.3 The Game Master operation
The celebrated case. Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:2125`: "When Agent Zero came for him [Game Master], the Game Master turned to the Hierarchy for protection. Xeth'Raal the Debt Collector drafted a contract: safety guaranteed, in exchange for the Goggles upon the Game Master's destruction. The Hierarchy honored every clause — then sent Agent Zero the Game Master's own strategic playbook, rendering his forty-two escape routes into forty-two dead ends." The Game Master destruction at Zenon is canonically Xeth'Raal's most famous orchestration.

### 2.4 "The contract guaranteed safety, not secrecy"
Per `hierarchyCanon.ts:127`: Xeth'Raal's canonical signature line. The operation pivoted on a deliberate clause-asymmetry — the Game Master signed for safety; the Hierarchy honored safety; the Hierarchy then sent Agent Zero the playbook, which the contract did NOT prohibit. The signature line is load-bearing for understanding Xeth'Raal's method: he does not break contracts; he weaponizes their margins.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Debt Collector title
Xeth'Raal's working title. Canonically not honorary — he canonically COLLECTS debts the Hierarchy is owed across the souled and unsouled economies. "The Debt Collector" is how Agent Zero / Vex Solène's faction canonically refers to him; "the CFO" is how the Hierarchy refers to him internally.

### 3.2 The Mol'Garath chain of command
Mol'Garath is canonically the Hierarchy's COO-or-CEO equivalent; Xeth'Raal reports up through Mol'Garath. The reporting line is canonical — Xeth'Raal does NOT operate independently of the Hierarchy's senior leadership, even on the Game Master operation.

### 3.3 The Goggles
The asset acquired in the Game Master operation. Per the Game Master canon, the Goggles are an artifact of strategic foresight — the Game Master could canonically read combat outcomes via them. Xeth'Raal canonically possesses the Goggles post-destruction, though their current location and use are partially canon-pending.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 Mol'Garath
The chain. Xeth'Raal reports to Mol'Garath; Mol'Garath assigns Xeth'Raal the high-value operations. Their working relationship is canonical mutual respect — Mol'Garath canonically does not micromanage the CFO; Xeth'Raal canonically does not bypass the chain.

### 4.2 Mol'Vereth
The peer. Mol'Vereth handles instruments; Xeth'Raal handles ledgers. They coordinate on multi-clause operations (`mystery.the_degen` E3; `mystery.game_master` protection contract). Functional respect.

### 4.3 The Game Master
The signature case. Canonically destroyed by the Game Master operation. Xeth'Raal canonically does NOT speak of the Game Master with malice — he speaks with the dryness of a banker citing a closed position. The Game Master's destruction was a settlement, not a vendetta.

### 4.4 Agent Zero / Vex Solène
The instrument's other side. Xeth'Raal canonically sent her the playbook; he canonically did NOT employ her. The cross-arc relationship is contractually-precise — Agent Zero was the beneficiary of the playbook delivery, not the Hierarchy's agent.

### 4.5 Brel'Sorrash
Hierarchy peer / subordinate (canon-pending exact position). Authoring should treat as a Hierarchy mid-tier officer Xeth'Raal works through.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 The Ledger of Ruin contents
Canon-pending and load-bearing. The Ledger's full contents are canonically opaque — only Xeth'Raal sees the entries. Authoring CANNOT reveal a specific Ledger entry.

### 6.2 The protection-contract method
Authoring trap. The canonical method is: HONOR every clause; weaponize the margins. Writers who portray Xeth'Raal as a contract-breaker have violated the canon. Xeth'Raal is canonically the most reliable signatory in the Hierarchy — and that reliability is the source of his menace.

### 6.3 The Goggles' current location
