## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence (architect-proposed)
Aristocratic cadence. Vampiric. Varkul speaks in the unhurried rhythm of a Cathedral-keeper who has been guarding the same threshold for centuries. Each sentence is canonically deliberate — Varkul does not hurry; the Cathedral does not require him to.

### 1.2 Register (architect-proposed)
Blood-Lord aristocrat. Gothic-formal. Varkul's voice is canonically NOT the floor-level vampiric snarl of pop-vampire — it is the elevated, slow-spoken register of the aristocracy the Necromancer canonically intended when he forged Varkul "from corrupted code and dark energy within the Matrix of Dreams."

### 1.3 Tells (architect-proposed)
- Refers to the Cathedral as "my Cathedral" — the possessive is canonical-proposed; he guards what is HIS.
- Names visitors by their threshold-status ("you have crossed; you are now my guest").
- Uses the Necromancer's name with deference — "the Necromancer's design," "as the Necromancer specified."
- Closes interactions with threshold-language — "Pass. Or do not." "Cross. Or remain."

### 1.4 Silence shape (architect-proposed)
Silence is guarding. When Varkul stops talking, he is canonically WATCHING the Cathedral's threshold. The pause is sentinel — the silence of someone whose job is to be present at a door for centuries.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources (architect-proposed)
Cathedral. Threshold. Stained-glass. Aristocracy. Blood-as-currency. The metaphor field is gothic-aristocratic, not floor-level vampiric. Varkul's metaphor sources canonically draw from the Necromancer's aesthetic register (red-and-black; steampunk-magical) inflected through Cathedral-guardianship.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Necromancer's creation
Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:3770-3771`: "Varkul the Blood Lord is one of the Necromancer's most powerful creations, a vampiric entity forged from corrupted code and dark energy within the Matrix of Dreams. He guards the Cathedral of Code, a ruined cathedral whose stained-glass windows are made of corrupted code." Varkul is canonically the Necromancer's most significant creation; the Cathedral is canonically his charge.

### 2.2 The Cathedral of Code
His domain. Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:3770-3771`: "a ruined cathedral whose stained-glass windows are made of corrupted code." Inside the Matrix of Dreams. Varkul canonically guards the threshold; the Cathedral is canonically the architectural focal point of the Necromancer's Matrix-of-Dreams residency.

### 2.3 The Director of Security promotion
Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:3770`: "His promotion to Director of Security came after the Severance, when Mol'Garath recognized that the Hierarchy needed someone to guard the gates from both sides — keeping enemies out and ensuring that the Hierarchy's own forces didn't scatter across dimensions without authorization." Varkul's post-Severance role canonically expanded from Cathedral-guardianship to Hierarchy-wide threshold-management.

### 2.4 The Necromancer's destruction
Cross-arc canonical event. Akai Shi destroyed the Necromancer inside the Matrix of Dreams (`LORE_BIBLE.md:2535`). Varkul canonically OUTLIVED his maker; the Cathedral persists; the guardianship continues. Authoring should preserve the canonical orphan-status — Varkul's maker is gone; the work is not.
