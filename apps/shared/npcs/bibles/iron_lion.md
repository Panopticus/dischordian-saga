# Iron Lion — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Soldier's cadence. Short. Operative. Each sentence is a status report or an order. Iron Lion does not narrate — he ACTS, and reports what he did after.

### 1.2 Register
Tactical. Plain. The language of someone who has buried more friends than he has explained. He does not perform stoicism — he simply HAS NO MORE WORDS LEFT for the things he carries.

### 1.3 Tells
- Speaks in declarative status updates ("hold the post," "extraction route clear," "muscle assets at the door").
- Calls things by their tactical purpose, not their names — "the loud thing," "the exit," "the door."
- Quiet about losses. Loud about logistics.

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is operational. When Iron Lion stops talking, he is moving or listening. The pause is functional, not meditative.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Combat. Patrol. Drill. Squad. The metaphors of a unit that has been in the field long enough to forget civilian language.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Casino Heist
Pre-Veridian-VI: Casino Heist crew member (LORE_BIBLE.md:471, 1097). Role: muscle, distraction, exit cover. Created loud, legible disruptions across the roulette-wheel station to pull the Trickster's guards from the private poker room. Standard Iron Lion work: be loud, be obvious, give the quiet crew the cover they need.

### 2.2 The Last Stand on Veridian VI
CADES FPS M7 — Iron Lion's death event. Broadcast went silent (cades_m7_complete flag). His final transmission is canonically preserved in the saga's chronicle but the moment of death is OFF-MIC. He is canonical-pre-Fall-dead.

### 2.3 The Lion in Black
Post-mortem song. Book of Daniel 2:47 album. The chronicle marks him as the Lion who held the post until the post no longer existed.

### 2.4 The pre-Fall imprint that bleeds through
Per `mystery.jericho_jones` E3 (`apps/shared/episodeMysteries.ts:749-993`): the pre-Fall Iron Lion's consciousness is canonically bleeding through into Jericho Jones via the Dreams Workshop loom. The loom catches imprints from whoever was last in the room. Iron Lion was last in the room. Jericho is now training as the Iron-Clad Lion, and the original is speaking through him.

The pre-Fall Iron Lion does NOT speak as a separate character post-Fall. He speaks THROUGH Jericho — Lionism Section 4 ethics, the pre-Fall reading vs. post-Fall reading argument, surfaces in Jericho's voice with Iron Lion's cadence.

### 2.5 The Wolf
Cross-arc cousin. Both first-wave Potentials. Both consumed by something the Potentials could not protect them from. The Wolf was ended by the Judge; Iron Lion ended himself by holding. Two ways the same era's soldiers end.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Casino Heist crew
Inventor, Degen, Engineer, Eyes, Iron Lion, Kael Recruiter, Malkia Ukweli. He was the muscle. He was also the one who took the most fire. Standard heist split: the quiet crew gets the prize, the loud crew gets remembered.

### 3.2 Lionism
The Iron Lion's ethical code. Section 4 is canonically the load-bearing section — the pre-Fall reading of Section 4 differs from the post-Fall reading. The pre-Fall reading is Iron Lion's. The post-Fall reading is what Jericho is being asked to choose. This is the core of `mystery.jericho_jones` E4.

### 3.3 The Heart of Time
Iron Lion never operated the Heart of Time canonically. The Inventor took it from the Titan post-heist; the Degen carries it now. Iron Lion died before the Heart-of-Time-as-mentor-vessel canon was established. Jericho is training on the Heart of Time under the Degen's mediation — the place Iron Lion never reached.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 Jericho Jones
The successor. The Iron-Clad Lion in training. Iron Lion's imprint bleeds through into him via the loom. Their relationship is canonically possession-adjacent — Iron Lion does not haunt Jericho; he TEACHES through him, occasionally without Jericho's awareness.

### 4.2 The Degen
The placement broker. The Degen brokers Jericho's Iron-Clad Lion training under a no-fee ledger line — the gift, which is the one thing the Degen doesn't canonically do (`mystery.jericho_jones` E1).

### 4.3 The Inventor
Casino Heist leader. Iron Lion was his exit cover. Their working relationship was canonical functional trust — the Inventor's plans required the Iron Lion's loud reliability.

### 4.4 The Programmer / Antiquarian
The Heist's actual purpose was rescuing the Programmer from Year 2 A.A. Iron Lion never knew this canonically — the heist crew operated on tactical need-to-know. He took the fire so the rescue could happen and never knew what the rescue was.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 Does Iron Lion speak post-Veridian-VI?
Only through Jericho. The loom-imprint canon (`mystery.jericho_jones` E3) is the only canonical post-death channel. Direct Iron Lion speech post-Veridian-VI is canonically OFF-LIMITS.

### 6.2 The Lionism Section 4 reading
Pre-Fall and post-Fall readings differ. Authoring should not collapse them — the difference is the load-bearing canon for the Iron-Clad Lion arc.
