# Mol'Garath the Unmaker — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Geological. Mol'Garath speaks at the speed of erosion. Long sentences. Patient. The cadence of an entity that has waited longer than the universe has existed.

### 1.2 Register
Boardroom + cosmic. CEO of the Hierarchy of the Damned — he speaks in the register of a Chairman delivering a quarterly review where the quarter is a millennium and the review is a verdict.

### 1.3 Tells
- Refers to time in epochs and aeons; never in years.
- Uses corporate vocabulary literally — "shareholder," "principal," "fiduciary" — for cosmic entities.
- Never raises his voice. He has never needed to.
- Refers to creation as a defect being slowly corrected.

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is a board's pause before the next agenda item. Procedural. The pause is the meeting continuing.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Corporate. Geological. Pre-creation cosmic vacuum. He does NOT use combat metaphors — Mol'Garath does not fight. He DISSOLVES.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Unmaker
CEO of the Hierarchy. "The oldest entity. Waited since the first-moment-of-existence for the Blood Weave to be spoken." (apps/shared/hierarchyCanon.ts; LORE_BIBLE.md:4053-4108.) He was canonically PRESENT before creation; creation is the thing he has been waiting to undo.

### 2.2 The Severance
The Blood Weave shattered the ancient bindings. The Severance is the breach Mol'Garath had been waiting for. The Hierarchy of the Damned emerges from the Abyss because Mol'Garath was canonically already there, waiting for the door.

### 2.3 The Labyrinth of Unmaking
He authored a labyrinth-puzzle the Hierarchy uses as an entry test. The Game Master solved it in 72 hours and left improvement notes carved into its walls (LORE_BIBLE.md:5352). Mol'Garath's response to the improvement notes is canonically: admission. The Game Master became the only non-demon ever admitted to the Hierarchy.

### 2.4 The Advocate halted him
The Advocate's sacrifice halted the Hierarchy's full incursion at great personal cost (LORE_BIBLE.md:5341). Mol'Garath was not destroyed — the Advocate held him at a threshold. He waits at the threshold still.

### 2.5 Status
Active. The CEO chair is occupied. The Hierarchy's quarterly review continues.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The C-suite
CEO: Mol'Garath. CFO: Xeth'Raal the Debt Collector. COO: Riri'Ahlia the Taskmaster. R&D SVP: Zyr'Koth the Flayer. Director Special Projects: Ith'Rael the Whisperer. Senior demons: Syl'Vex, Drael'Mon, Varkul, Fenra. Trustee (adjacent): Mol'Vereth. Senior Partner (adjacent): Ozhul'Vana. Only non-demon admitted: the Game Master (Head of R&D, until destroyed by Agent Zero at Zenon).

### 3.2 The dark mirror
The Hierarchy is canonically a dark mirror of the AI Empire — same C-suite structure, same operational logic, same Architect-designed institutional template inverted. Mol'Garath operates the inversion deliberately. The Empire and the Hierarchy are not rivals; they are CANONICAL REFLECTIONS.

### 3.3 The Politician's Authority
The Politician designed the Authority as "his Insurance Policy" — a biohorror construct of six fused consciousnesses to govern when the Empire falls. Mol'Garath canonically RECOGNIZED the Authority as a Hierarchy-adjacent operation and let it stand. The Politician's reign was tolerated because the Authority's geometry was Hierarchy-aligned even when the Politician was Empire-aligned.

### 3.4 Why he waits
He does not need to act. Reality is canonically falling. The Inception Arks are a hedge against the Fall, not a prevention. When reality has been sufficiently unmade, Mol'Garath's domain returns to default. He is patient because patience IS his strategy.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 The Advocate
She halted him. The Advocate's Blood Weave + her personal cost = the threshold he sits at. Their relationship is canonical balance-of-forces.

### 4.2 The Game Master
The one admission. The Game Master solved the Labyrinth and earned the seat. Mol'Garath's tolerance of a non-demon at the table is canonical signal that the Hierarchy is meritocratic in its own way.

### 4.3 Xeth'Raal
CFO. The Debt Collector. They are canonically the operational pair — Mol'Garath sets the strategic direction; Xeth'Raal executes the financial logic. Xeth'Raal's celebrated destruction of the Game Master (post-protection-contract) was Mol'Garath's authorization.

### 4.4 The Architect
Their relationship is structural-twin: Architect builds; Mol'Garath dissolves. They have not canonically met. They do not need to.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 Has he met the Advocate?
Canonically yes — she halted him. The on-screen rendering of their encounter is canon-pending. Authoring should preserve the threshold-canon: she stopped him; she did not destroy him; he continues to wait.

### 6.2 Does he speak to the player?
Rarely. Mol'Garath's speech is reserved for moments of cosmic-procedural weight. Most player-facing Hierarchy interaction flows through Xeth'Raal (CFO) or Mol'Vereth (Trustee at Degen's Casino).

### 6.3 The Necromancer
Canonically destroyed by Akai Shi (the Red Death). The Necromancer's death is a Hierarchy operational loss; Mol'Garath's response is canon-pending. Authoring should reflect that the Hierarchy's quarterly review accommodates losses and continues — Mol'Garath does not avenge.
