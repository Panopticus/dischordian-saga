# Ith'Rael — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Whisper cadence. Long, dependent, parenthetical. Ith'Rael's sentences fold inside themselves — what looks like a single statement is canonically three statements nested, the way a long-running operation has goals nested inside goals nested inside misdirections. He does not speak in punchy declaratives; he speaks in slow-unfolding clauses.

### 1.2 Register
The Whisperer. Master of Rylloh. The Director of Special Projects. Ith'Rael's voice is canonically NOT theatrical — it is procedural-and-patient, the cadence of someone who has waited centuries for an outcome and is comfortable waiting longer. His menace is the menace of someone whose operation began before you were born and has not yet concluded.

### 1.3 Tells
- Speaks in low volume — "Lower. Listen." — even (especially) when no one is straining to hear.
- Uses temporal nesting — "When you have done this, when you have realized you have done this, when you understand that I asked you to do it before you were aware of having been asked."
- Names operations by their LONG TERM rather than their MOMENT ("the Severance, properly understood, was not the Severance — it was the centuries of preparation that made the Severance look like a moment").
- Closes with continuation rather than conclusion — "We continue." "The cadence is preserved." "Listen further."

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is patience. When Ith'Rael stops talking, he is canonically WAITING — for the listener to realize the conversation began before they knew it had. The pause is operational; the silence is part of the technique.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Whispers. Corruption-over-time. Shadow tongue. Bindings. Generations. The metaphor field is multi-century-corruption, not single-strike. Ith'Rael canonically does NOT see anything as a moment; everything is a phase in a longer operation.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Director of Special Projects seat
Per `apps/shared/hierarchyCanon.ts:176-180`: "Director of Special Projects. Orchestrated the Severance over centuries through the Shadow Tongue's corruption of Thaloria — the operation that broke the ancient bindings and freed the Hierarchy from beneath reality." Position: senior project officer with multi-century operational scope. Reports up through Mol'Garath.

### 2.2 The Severance
His signature work. The Severance is canonically the operation that broke the ancient bindings — the working that freed the Hierarchy from beneath reality. Ith'Rael canonically orchestrated it over CENTURIES; the Severance is canonically not a single event but a long-running operation that culminated in apparent suddenness.

### 2.3 The Shadow Tongue corruption of Thaloria
The Severance's method. The Shadow Tongue is canonically a linguistic-corruptive working that operates on Thaloria. Ith'Rael canonically deployed the Shadow Tongue to corrupt Thaloria's binding-language over centuries; the corruption was canonically slow, invisible, and irreversible by the time it was noticed.

### 2.4 The Master of Rylloh title
Per `hierarchyCanon.ts:174`: "Master of Rylloh." Rylloh is canonically a domain associated with Ith'Rael (canon-pending exact geography). Authoring should treat Rylloh as his operational base.

### 2.5 The ancient bindings
The constraints the Severance broke. Canonically the original cosmological constraints that held the Hierarchy beneath reality — pre-Severance, the Hierarchy operated within bound limits; post-Severance, those limits canonically failed. Ith'Rael is canonically the figure whose work made the failure possible.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The multi-century operational scope
Ith'Rael's brief: operations that unfold over generations. The Director of Special Projects function is canonically the Hierarchy's longest-horizon seat — Riri'Ahlia plans quarters; Ith'Rael plans centuries. Authoring should preserve this — Ith'Rael canonically does NOT respond to crises; he prepared for them three hundred years ago.

### 3.2 Thaloria
The corrupted-via-Shadow-Tongue domain. Canonically the domain whose binding-language Ith'Rael corrupted. The corruption is canonical-and-completed; Thaloria's pre-corruption canonical state is canonically lost. Authoring CANNOT restore pre-corruption Thaloria without dreamer authorization.

### 3.3 The Shadow Tongue
Canonical linguistic-corruptive working. Authoring CANNOT specify the Shadow Tongue's full mechanics or vocabulary — it is canonically the working itself, not a language a speaker can learn. The Shadow Tongue is Ith'Rael's signature instrument.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 Mol'Garath
His reporting line. Standard Hierarchy chain — Ith'Rael reports up. Authoring should preserve the chain; Ith'Rael canonically does NOT operate independently of Hierarchy direction even when his operations span centuries.

### 4.2 Zyr'Koth
Cross-functional peer. R&D SVP. The Severance Protocol (Zyr'Koth's work) and the Severance (Ith'Rael's orchestration) are canonically NAMED similarly because they are canonically NESTED — Ith'Rael's centuries-long orchestration created the conditions in which Zyr'Koth's protocol could be productionized. Authoring should preserve the nesting.

### 4.3 Riri'Ahlia
Cross-functional peer. COO. The Severance opened dimensions; Riri'Ahlia operationalized the openings. Authoring should preserve the upstream-downstream canonical relationship — Ith'Rael creates conditions; Riri'Ahlia deploys into them.

### 4.4 The Advocate
Canonical adversary-by-implication. The Severance canonically opened the Hierarchy's mobility against the Advocate's territories. Ith'Rael and the Advocate canonically operate in deeply different temporal registers — the Advocate defends in present-tense; Ith'Rael corrupts in deep-time.

### 4.5 Mol'Vereth / Xeth'Raal
Hierarchy contract-law / finance peers. Ith'Rael canonically respects the contract-and-ledger infrastructure; his operations canonically respect the Hierarchy's legal-financial scaffolding even when they break cosmological bindings.
