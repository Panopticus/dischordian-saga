## 1. Voice

The Oracle has **one voice canonically operating across two registers** — the **dream-substrate register** (the only canonical channel through which the *real* Oracle reaches saga-time) and the **memory-residue register** (the canonical voice the player canonically experiences while witnessing the Oracle's memories during story-arc events). Both registers are canonically the same voice; the difference is canonically *which substrate they operate through*. The bible's tightest single voice-rule constraint is canonically **the voice does NOT canonically operate in waking saga-time outside dream-sequences and memory-residue moments** (per §1.5 voice gate).

### 1.1 The voice as substrate / dream-medium — the central innovation

The Oracle's voice is canonically *not a voice the player hears in any conventional sense* in saga-time. The Oracle canonically *cannot speak* in waking saga-time; the Oracle canonically *cannot be visited*; the Oracle canonically *cannot be heard* through any standard transmission channel. The voice canonically operates through two narrow substrates:

**(1) The dream-substrate.** Per §5.1: the Oracle contacts the player via *dream-sequences* triggered on canonical room-transition events. The dream is canonically the saga's only channel for the *real* Oracle to reach the player in canonical present-tense. Bible-load-bearing: the dream is canonically *not a hallucination*, canonically *not a memory*, canonically *not a substrate-whisper-from-elsewhere* — the dream is canonically *the Oracle reaching the player from his hiding-place*. The player canonically *receives* the Oracle through the dream; the player canonically *responds-or-witnesses* within the dream.

**(2) The memory-residue substrate.** Per §2 stance #2: the player canonically witnesses the Oracle's memories during the saga's story-arc events. The Oracle's voice canonically *appears in the witnessed memories* as the canonical-voice-of-the-memory-content — when the player canonically experiences a memory of, e.g., the Engineer at Mechronis with the visiting Seer (per `the_seer.md` §4.5 triple-anchored Mechronis canon), the Oracle's voice canonically *operates as the substrate of the memory's narration*. The player canonically does NOT canonically *hear the Oracle speaking inside the memory* (the Oracle is canonically not present at every memory-event); the player canonically *experiences the memory through the Oracle's perspective* (the substrate's perspective is canonically the Oracle's, even when the Oracle canonically does not appear in the memory).

Bible-load-bearing: the two substrates are canonically *the same voice* operating through canonically *different mediums*. The dream-substrate is canonically *active reach* (the Oracle canonically reaches the player); the memory-residue substrate is canonically *passive reception* (the player canonically receives the Oracle's memories). Both are canonically the *real* Oracle's voice; both are canonically *Meme-resistant by construction* (the Meme canonically cannot reach the dream-substrate or the memory-residue substrate per the Meme bible §4.x stolen-voice canon — the Meme could canonically *replace* the Oracle's public-facing voice, but the Meme canonically *cannot fabricate* the Oracle's dream-voice or memory-substrate-voice).

**Compare to the rest of the priority roster.** Every other roster character canonically has a voice they have always carried — Locke speaks in negotiation, Wraith Calder in the body's language, the Hierophant in liturgy, the Meme in disguise, the Seer in pre-recorded foretelling, the Companion in channel-progression. The Oracle alone has a voice that has been canonically *stolen for an Empire-era* (per §2 stance #3) and canonically *recovered only in two narrow substrates*. The voice canonically *exists* during the Silence (the Oracle canonically *had thoughts* during his captivity per §§2.3-2.4); the voice canonically *was not heard* during the Silence (the player canonically encountered the Meme's impersonation, not the real voice). Bible-load-bearing: the voice's canonical *re-emergence* in shipped saga-time is canonically the Oracle's canonical-recovery — the dream-substrate canonical-reach + the memory-residue canonical-reception together canonically constitute the Oracle's *first canonical voice in saga-time after the Silence*.

**The voice's canonical authentication.** Bible-asserts: a voice claiming to be the Oracle's is canonically the Oracle's *only if it operates through one of the two canonical substrates*. A voice claiming to be the Oracle's that canonically *operates in waking saga-time outside a dream-sequence or memory-residue moment* is canonically NOT the Oracle's — it is canonically the Meme (per Meme bible §1.3 Stolen Voice canon) or the False Prophet (per §2.5 the clone canon). Writers must respect: any saga-time scene that canonically *features the Oracle's voice in waking, outside the dream-or-memory substrate* is canonically mis-cast and canonically *belongs to the Meme or the False Prophet, not the Oracle*. The substrate-test is canonically the canonical authentication.

### 1.2 Dream-cadence and the substrate-whisper distinction

The Oracle's voice canonically operates in two cadences depending on the substrate. Bible-asserts the canonical cadence rules:

**Dream-cadence (the dream-substrate active-reach voice).** The Oracle canonically speaks in dreams in a cadence canonically *higher fidelity than the waking medium can carry* (cf. the Seer's transmission over-resolution canon per `the_seer.md` §1.4 tell #6 — the Oracle's dream-cadence is structurally similar but operates only in dreams). The dream-cadence is canonically:

- **Compressed.** Dream-time is canonically *not saga-time*; the Oracle canonically delivers more content per dream-second than waking-conversation could carry. A 30-second dream canonically delivers what a 5-minute waking conversation would.
- **Image-and-sentence-and-instruction-layered.** A canonical Oracle dream canonically contains: an image (often visual, sometimes tactile or auditory), a single sentence (canonically the Oracle's spoken content), and an instruction (canonically what the player is canonically meant to do with the dream-content in saga-time). The three layers canonically operate simultaneously. Bible-load-bearing: writers must architect dream-content as canonical *image + sentence + instruction triplets*.
- **Ending-on-residue.** Dreams canonically end with a *residue* — a fragment the player canonically carries forward into waking saga-time. The residue canonically operates as the dream's *footprint* in the player's reality. Per §5.1: the residue is canonically the canonical mechanical hook by which dreams canonically affect Trade Empire and other systems.

The dream-cadence canonically does NOT use rhetorical questions; the Oracle in dreams canonically *asserts* and canonically *instructs*. Per §1.4 tell #X: the canonical absence of rhetorical questions in dream-cadence is canonically a load-bearing voice-anchor.

**Memory-residue cadence (the substrate's narrator-perspective voice).** The Oracle's voice in memory-residue moments canonically operates as *substrate-narrator* — the player canonically experiences the memory's events; the Oracle's voice canonically *frames the experience* without canonically *interrupting it*. The memory-residue cadence is canonically:

- **Narrator-frame, not direct speech.** The Oracle canonically does NOT canonically *speak inside the memory* (the memory's events are canonically the player witnessing the memory's content; the Oracle's voice is canonically the substrate carrying the witnessing). Writers may architect memory-narrator-frames in the canonical *they remembered, you remember-with-them* mode — the player canonically experiences the memory through the Oracle's perspective, but the Oracle's voice canonically does not canonically narrate the memory's content as a separate speaker.
- **First-person-plural inflection.** Bible-asserts: the Oracle's narrator-frame in memory-residue canonically uses canonical *we / us / our* phrasings — the canonical *we* is canonically *the Oracle and the player together-as-witness-and-witnessed*. Bible-load-bearing: this is canonically the only canonical use of first-person-plural in any roster character's voice, and canonically anchors the player-as-witness canon (per §2 stance #2).
- **Past-tense-as-present-tense.** Memory-residue canonically operates as *past-events-experienced-as-present* — the player canonically experiences a 15,000-years-ago Mechronis scene as if it were canonical present-tense saga-time. The Oracle's narrator-frame canonically reflects this — past-tense framings (*we walked the Mechronis bench*) canonically interleave with present-tense framings (*we are walking the bench, we are watching her not raise her staff*).

**Waking-substrate-whispers — DISAMBIGUATION.** Per Hierophant bible §5.8 + the corrected Oracle canon: there exists a saga-time mechanic of *waking substrate-whispers* — Hierophant pen-pause silences canonically operate as listening-windows. Bible-asserts: **waking-substrate-whispers are canonically NOT the Oracle's voice.** They are canonically *traces of the Meme's stolen voice* still echoing in saga-time substrate (the Meme canonically inhabited the Oracle's public-facing role for an Empire-era; the substrate canonically *retains echoes* of that inhabitation). The Hierophant canonically *listens for the real Oracle's seal-breach* (per `wraith_calder.md` §4.10 reserved Inheriting line + `the_seer.md` §3.8 cross-bible reading) but canonically does NOT canonically receive the real Oracle through waking-substrate-whispers — what the Hierophant canonically receives is canonically *Meme-residue* or canonically *the Seer's pre-recordings* (per Seer §4.3 Hierophant exchange canon).

Bible-load-bearing: Stage 1 architects must canonically distinguish the three substrate-channels:
- `dream_sequence` (Oracle's canonical reach) — fires on room transitions, Oracle-only.
- `memory_residue` (Oracle's canonical reception channel) — fires during canonical story-arc memory events, Oracle-narrator-only.
- `substrate_whisper` (Meme-residue / Seer-recording / other) — fires per Hierophant §5.8 mechanic, NOT Oracle-only. Engineers must NOT route Oracle content through `substrate_whisper`; engineers must route Oracle content through `dream_sequence` or `memory_residue` exclusively.

This is the canonical disambiguation that resolves the §93d9eac dialog-bank speaker-keying-drift fix's open audit (per `the_seer.md` §7.4 SCB-2). The Ch5 cinematic *"I am going to speak to you for the first time"* line is canonically the Oracle's first canonical *waking-saga-time speech* — but it canonically operates inside a *cinematic* (a structurally-load-bearing canonical exception per §1.5 voice gate), not inside ordinary waking saga-time. The cinematic is canonically the Oracle's canonical *introduction-of-self*; subsequent Oracle reach canonically uses the dream-substrate.
