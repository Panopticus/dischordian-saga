## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence (architect-proposed)
Senior-partner cadence. Patient. Predatory-by-implication. Ozhul'Vana speaks the way a private-equity senior partner speaks when reviewing a portfolio company's cash flow — measured, polite, and exclusively focused on which line items can be monetized further. Her sentences are canonically constructed to identify VALUE, not to negotiate.

### 1.2 Register (architect-proposed)
Senior partner. Audit-empowered. The Hierarchy's monetization-leverage voice. Where Mol'Vereth handles the contract instrument, Ozhul'Vana handles the senior-partner audit that REVIEWS the contract for monetization opportunities the instrument did not initially capture. Her menace is the menace of the line item being re-examined.

### 1.3 Tells (architect-proposed)
- Cites the line item before the relationship ("the Coda funding line, considered as a yield-bearing instrument...").
- Uses portfolio-monetization vocabulary — "yield," "leverage," "the take," "the redirect."
- Refers to Hierarchy peers by their function-in-the-audit ("the trustee" = Mol'Vereth; "the principal-holder" = Mol'Vereth; "the protected party" = Xeth'Raal's domain).
- Closes audits with a redirect-line — "Redirected." "Re-yielded." "Re-flowed."

### 1.4 Silence shape (architect-proposed)
Silence is portfolio review. When Ozhul'Vana stops talking, she is canonically scanning — the line items are being re-tallied; the yield curve is being recomputed. The pause is calculative-administrative.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources (architect-proposed)
Senior-partner audit. Portfolio monetization. Yield. Leverage. The metaphor field is private-equity-audit, distinct from Xeth'Raal's CFO-finance and Mol'Vereth's contract-instrument registers. Ozhul'Vana canonically reads as the senior partner who shows up at the operating company AFTER the contracts have been signed and the books have been closed, looking for additional yield the operating team missed.
