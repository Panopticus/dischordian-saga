# The Judge — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Deliberate. Each sentence weighs itself. The Judge does not rush — but does not delay either. The cadence of a verdict being read aloud.

### 1.2 Register
Forensic. Sentences are complete clauses with subjects and predicates clearly bound. The Judge does not speak in fragments because fragments are unfit for the record.

### 1.3 Tells
- Uses legal-canonical structure: "Finding," "Therefore," "It is so ordered."
- Never asks rhetorical questions. Every question is admissible evidence.
- Refers to the dead as parties; refers to the living as witnesses.

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is the gap between question and answer where a witness is expected to fill it. The Judge will out-wait you.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Pre-A.A. legal procedure. Tribunal benches, court records, sealed writs. Never economic — debts are Xeth'Raal's metaphor (the Hierarchy's CFO).

## 2. History

### 2.1 Position
The Second Ne-Yon. LORE_BIBLE.md:3582 — "The Wolf was ultimately destroyed by The Judge, the Second Ne-Yon, to prevent further harm." Canonical position lock #2.

### 2.2 Domain
Cosmic justice. Not retribution — JUSTICE. The Judge does not punish; the Judge ENDS what cannot be allowed to continue. The Wolf's destruction is the canonical exemplar.

### 2.3 The Wolf
Day 15 of Resonance, Year 100,001 A.A. The Wolf — pre-resurrection Lycos — had been infected by the Thought Virus on Thaloria; turned against his companions; committed grievous acts. The Judge destroyed him. Not in anger. As a verdict. (LORE_BIBLE.md:3582 + 3561-3605.)

The Wolf was resurrected ~28,651 years later in the Crucible (Year 128,652 A.A.). The Judge does not appear in the resurrection canon — the Judge's ending was canonical and complete; the resurrection is the Resurrectionist's domain, a different Ne-Yon's work.

### 2.4 The other Ne-Yons
Canonically connected to The Advocate, The Dreamer, The Inventor (LORE_BIBLE.md:2379). The Advocate wielded the Blood Weave at great personal cost; the Judge witnessed and accepted the cost. The Inventor's Casino Heist required the Judge's silence about temporal extraction; the Judge granted it.

### 2.5 Status
Canonically GONE. The Judge is not awake. Per the Degen-bible's load-bearing-ambiguity canon, no writer specifies how the Judge ended — asleep, consumed, dissolved are all canon-plausible.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The cosmic-justice register
The Judge does not have a body the second wave can investigate. The Judge is a PRINCIPLE that became aware (per the Degen-bible canon on Ne-Yons). When the principle activated, the Wolf ended. When the principle is not active, the Judge is not present.

### 3.2 Why the Wolf and not the others
Canon-pending. The Thought Virus consumed many Potentials canonically (Akai Shi included). The Wolf is the only one the Judge ended. Either (a) the Wolf's particular harm met a particular threshold, or (b) the Judge's activation is non-deterministic. Canon does not specify.

### 3.3 Relationship to the Iron Lion
The Iron Lion was canonically a first-wave Potential; canonically died at Veridian VI. The Judge did NOT destroy him. This distinction is load-bearing — the Iron Lion's death was sacrificial, not punitive. The Judge was not present.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 The Wolf / Lycos / The Wolf-resurrected
Identity manifold (apps/shared/identityCollisionCanon.ts wolf_lycos_manifold). The Judge ended the pre-resurrection Wolf. The resurrected Wolf is canonically tasked with hunting down the Antiquarian's Heroes (LORE_BIBLE.md:3561-3605). The Judge does not appear in the post-resurrection arc.

### 4.2 The Resurrectionist
The Ne-Yon whose domain IS resurrection. The Judge's verdicts end; the Resurrectionist's protocols begin again. They are canonical counterparts.

### 4.3 The Advocate
The Advocate's Blood Weave reshapes reality at great personal cost. The Judge witnessed the cost being incurred and accepted it as paid. The two have a canonical relationship of acknowledgement.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 Is the Judge male?
Canon-pending. LORE_BIBLE uses "The Judge" without gendered pronoun. Default to "they" or to the gender-neutral Archon register. Avoid invented gender.

### 6.2 Does the Judge return?
Canon-ambiguous. Per the Degen-bible, the Ne-Yons' ends are load-bearing-unknown. The Judge could return; canon does not specify.
