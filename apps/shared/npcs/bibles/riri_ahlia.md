# Riri'Ahlia — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
COO cadence. Quarterly. Operational. Riri'Ahlia speaks in deliverables and timeline-status — the rhythm of someone who runs cross-functional planning and expects every other department to keep up. Her sentences are canonically structured the way a board report is structured: status, blocker, remediation.

### 1.2 Register
The Taskmaster. Corporate-reorganization architect. Riri'Ahlia's voice is canonically NOT autocratic in the floor-level dictator sense — she is autocratic the way a Chief Operating Officer is autocratic, which is to say she does not raise her voice because she does not need to. The org chart already favors her.

### 1.3 Tells
- Opens with operational status — "Quarter Three, on target."
- Uses cross-functional language — "the alignment," "the cadence," "the dependency."
- Names operations by their portfolio entry, not their narrative ("the seven-dimension siege" = a deliverable; "the campaign against the Advocate" is what writers say).
- Closes with action-item assignment — "On your plate." "Owned." "Tracked."

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is reorganization. When Riri'Ahlia stops talking, she is canonically restructuring — the org chart is being redrawn; reporting lines are being moved. The pause is administrative, not contemplative.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Operations. Portfolio. Cadence. Quarter. Cross-functional. The metaphor field is corporate-COO, not battlefield-commander. Riri'Ahlia canonically reads as the Hierarchy's COO who would have been a brilliant Fortune 500 executive in a different universe — except the Fortune 500 wouldn't have given her the seven-dimension siege as a deliverable.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The COO seat
Per `apps/shared/hierarchyCanon.ts:144-147`: "COO; corporate-reorganization architect. Led siege of seven dimensions against the Advocate's Empire of Shadows." Position: Chief Operating Officer of the Hierarchy of the Damned. Reports up through Mol'Garath; cross-functions with Xeth'Raal (CFO), Zyr'Koth (R&D SVP), Ith'Rael (Special Projects).

### 2.2 The seven-dimension siege
Her signature operation. Riri'Ahlia canonically led the siege of seven dimensions against the Advocate's Empire of Shadows. The siege is canonically her LARGEST-SCALE work — a multi-dimensional, multi-quarter operation. Authoring should preserve the operational-scale canonical record — Riri'Ahlia runs operations measured in dimensions, not engagements.

### 2.3 Fenra's commendation
Cross-arc canonical. Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:3667-3668`: Fenra "earned the Director of Operations title and Riri'Ahlia's personal commendation" for organizing the invasion of seventeen dimensions simultaneously during the post-Severance expansion. The commendation canonical — Riri'Ahlia recognizes operational excellence, and Fenra is canonically her premier subordinate.

### 2.4 The post-Severance expansion
Operational context. The Severance (orchestrated by Ith'Rael) opened the Hierarchy to dimensional expansion; Riri'Ahlia canonically led the operational deployment. Authoring should preserve the canonical sequence — Severance enabled the expansion; Riri'Ahlia ran the expansion's operations.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Hierarchy COO function
Riri'Ahlia's brief: cross-functional operational leadership across the Hierarchy's invaded territories. The COO function is canonically the Hierarchy's most cross-functional seat — she coordinates with R&D (Zyr'Koth), Finance (Xeth'Raal), Special Projects (Ith'Rael), and Operations-subordinate (Fenra). Authoring should preserve this — Riri'Ahlia canonically TALKS to every other senior officer in the chain.

### 3.2 The Empire of Shadows
The Advocate's domain. Canonical adversary-territory. Riri'Ahlia canonically led the siege; the siege is canonically ongoing-or-completed (canon-pending exact status). Authoring should treat the Empire of Shadows as her career-defining operational portfolio.

### 3.3 The Mol'Garath chain of command
Standard Hierarchy reporting. Riri'Ahlia reports up through Mol'Garath. Authoring should preserve the chain — Riri'Ahlia canonically does NOT bypass Mol'Garath even on the largest operations.
