## Test Design

### Format

For each bible, a *blind-read attribution test* canonically operates as follows:

1. **Reviewer reads the canonical bible** (the §§1–3 voice + history + background sections as the canonical-load-bearing canonical-onboarding-content).
2. **Reviewer is presented with a mixed-line corpus**: 10 canonical lines, of which:
   - 5 are canonically *authored from this bible* (per §6 canonical voice samples + bible-derivable canonical-content).
   - 5 are canonically *authored from other roster characters* (per other bibles' §6 canonical voice samples).
3. **Reviewer attributes each line** to *Bible-X* or *not-Bible-X*. The lines are presented without canonical-name-attribution; the reviewer canonically applies canonical voice-rules from the bible to canonically distinguish.
4. **Pass criterion**: reviewer canonically attributes ≥9 of 10 correctly (90% accuracy).

### Special-case bibles with canonical-additional-disambiguation

Three bibles canonically operate canonical-additional canonical-disambiguation-axes that canonical-blind-read canonically tests:

- **Vex Solène** (per `vex_solene.md`): canonical four-stage reveal canonical-gate canon. Canonical blind-read canonically tests *both* canonical Vex-vs-non-Vex roster-disambiguation *and* canonical reveal-stage-disambiguation (canonical Vex-pre-reveal vs. canonical Vex-post-reveal). Reviewer canonically attributes lines to canonical-correct reveal-stage. Pass criterion: ≥9 of 10 + canonical-correct reveal-stage attribution per canonical-Vex-line.
- **The Meme** (per `the_meme.md`): canonical disguise-stratification canon. Canonical blind-read canonically tests canonical Meme-vs-non-Meme + canonical disguise-state-disambiguation. Reviewer canonically attributes canonical-correct canonical-disguise-active. Pass criterion: ≥9 of 10 + canonical-correct disguise attribution per canonical-Meme-line.
- **The Oracle** (per `the_oracle.md`): canonical voice-stolen-and-recovered canon + canonical-impersonator canonical disambiguation. Canonical blind-read canonically tests canonical Oracle-vs-non-Oracle + canonical Oracle-vs-Meme-impersonating-Oracle-vs-False-Prophet canonical-disambiguation. Reviewer canonically distinguishes canonical real-Oracle from canonical canonical-impersonator-content. Pass criterion: ≥9 of 10 + canonical ≥4-of-5 canonical-impersonator-disambiguation per canonical-mixed-Oracle-content corpus.

### Special-case bibles with canonical non-verbal expression

Two bibles canonically operate canonical *non-verbal-expression* canon (the Eidolon canonically permanently; the DMC Clone Body Companion canonically pre-naming):

- **The Eidolon** (per `eidolon.md` §5.x): canonical blind-read canonically tests canonical-mood-attribution-from-canonical-glyphs-alone (canonical visual-only test). Reviewer canonically reads 10 canonical Eidolon-canonical-glyph-overlays (canonical visual-renders) and canonically attributes canonical-mood. Pass criterion: ≥9 of 10 canonical-correct mood-attribution.
- **The DMC Clone Body Companion** (per `dmc_clone_companion.md` §1.1): canonical blind-read canonically tests canonical-channel-attribution. Reviewer canonically reads 10 canonical Companion-canonical-expressions (canonical mixed channels: glyphs, posture, sound, first-word, named-personality) and canonically attributes canonical-correct canonical-channel. Pass criterion: ≥9 of 10 canonical-correct channel-attribution.

### Special-case: trust-band attribution

For bibles with canonical multi-band trust-models (canonical Locke 3-band, canonical Hierophant 5-band, canonical Companion 4-band, canonical Oracle 4-band), canonical blind-read canonically additionally tests canonical-correct band-attribution per canonical-line. Pass criterion: ≥4-of-5 canonical-correct band-attribution per canonical-bible's canonical-bands.

## Pass criterion summary

A bible canonically passes the blind-read protocol when:
- Mixed-corpus attribution: ≥9 of 10 canonical-correct (90%).
- Trust-band / channel / register / state sub-test: ≥4 of 5 canonical-correct (80%).
- Special-case canonical-additional-disambiguation: ≥4 of 5 canonical-correct (80%).

A bible that canonically *does not* canonically pass: bible-content canonically requires canonical-revision before Stage 2 dialogue authoring canonically begins. Bible-content canonical-revision canonically operates per canonical-bible's canonical §6 voice samples + §1 voice canon.
