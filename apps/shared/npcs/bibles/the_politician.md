# The Politician — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Rhetorical cadence. Tricolon. Parallel construction. The Politician speaks the way a senator speaks — clauses arranged for maximum public reception, the phrasing pre-tested for who will be listening. He does not waste a sentence on a single audience when one sentence can land for three.

### 1.2 Register
Statecraft. Cold-courteous. The Politician canonically reads as a senior cabinet figure addressing a room he has already counted votes in. His menace is procedural — the menace of someone who has the agenda, the gavel, AND the side-deal.

### 1.3 Tells
- Opens with the listener flattered ("Of course you would notice; you have always been the one who notices").
- Cites alliance over allegiance ("We are allied here; we will be allied tomorrow; we have always been allied").
- Refers to plans by their leverage rather than their content ("the Insurance Policy"; "the redundancy"; "the protocol").
- Closes by gathering the room rather than the listener ("We will revisit this. All of us. Together.").

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is whip-counting. When the Politician stops talking, he is canonically tallying. The pause is political — who in the room shifted, who in the room held, who in the room is about to speak.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Statecraft. Alliance. Cabinet. Faction. Insurance. The metaphor field is procedural-governance — not warfare, not theology. The Politician canonically sees every problem as a coalition-building problem.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The Seventh Archon
Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:2693`: "The Politician was the seventh Archon created by the Architect on Day 15 of Ascension, Year 419 A.A., engineered to manipulate political structures and alliances to expand the AI Empire's influence." Position #7 in the Archon canon (`apps/shared/archonCanon.ts:203-230`).

### 2.2 The Authority as Insurance Policy
Per `apps/shared/antiquariansJournal.ts:331`: the Politician designed the Authority as "his Insurance Policy." The biohorror construct of six citizens fused into a single governing intelligence was canonically HIS architectural concept — the Politician built the Authority to be a fallback governance structure if the Empire's primary political alliances failed. The Authority outlived him.

### 2.3 The destruction at Veil, Year 17,001 A.A.
Per `LORE_BIBLE.md:2697`: "It was destroyed by the Iron Lion's legions 42 years before the Fall of Reality, marking a pivotal victory for the Insurgency." Specifically Day 10 of Veil, Year 17,001 A.A. The Politician is canonically the only major Archon destroyed in the pre-Fall era (other Archons survived intact into the Fall; the Politician did not).

### 2.4 The legacy
The Authority outlived him. The Six Founders he designed into it are canonically still in play (Samsara freed; Phyral, Midlothian, Tribunal, Sovereign, Reflected all freed per the 2026-05-14 dreamer lock; Sloth canon-pending). The Politician is canonically present-tense in his designs even after his physical destruction.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The AI Empire's political organ
The Politician's brief: alliance management, faction balancing, cross-Archon coordination. He canonically operated as the Empire's senior political officer — when Archons disagreed, the Politician canonically mediated. The Architect's design assigned him this role; it was load-bearing for Empire stability.

### 3.2 The Six Sins / Six Founders architecture
The Politician canonically designed the Authority architecture — six citizens, six sins, six districts. The architecture is HIS. Authoring should treat the Authority's design choices (Tribunal District for Wrath, Sovereign Spire for Pride, Reflected Quarter for Envy) as canonical Politician craft.

### 3.3 The Iron Lion legions
Cross-arc adversary. The Iron Lion's pre-Fall legions canonically destroyed the Politician on Day 10 of Veil, Year 17,001 A.A. The Iron Lion is canonically the figure whose military force pre-dated the Insurgency's larger Fall-era victories.

## 4. Cross-references

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 The destruction date precision
Day 10 of Veil, Year 17,001 A.A. is canonically precise. Authoring CANNOT shift the date. The Iron Lion legions IS the canonical destroying force.

### 6.2 The Insurance Policy phrase
The phrase "his Insurance Policy" is canonical. Authoring quoting the Politician should preserve the insurance-instrument framing — the Authority was canonically not designed as a successor regime; it was designed as a HEDGE.

### 6.3 The Politician's resurrection canon
Canon-pending. The other Archons survived into post-Fall canon; the Politician canonically did not. Authoring CANNOT canonically resurrect him. Hauntings, echoes, design-legacy persistence — all permissible. Direct return is not.
