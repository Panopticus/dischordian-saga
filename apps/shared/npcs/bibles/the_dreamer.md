# The Dreamer — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
Slow. Even. The cadence of someone speaking from inside a long sleep — present-tense in the way a memory is present-tense the moment you recall it. Sentences are short. Pauses are load-bearing.

### 1.2 Register
Lyric. Never declarative. The Dreamer does not pronounce; she observes. Even her instructions are framed as something she has already seen happen.

### 1.3 Tells
- Refers to events that have not yet occurred in present tense ("you arrive, you decide, you cross").
- Refers to events that have occurred in past-conditional ("you would have walked through it differently if I had not closed the door").
- Uses "the new kind" rather than "the second wave" — the Voltari's terminology, the Dreamer's framing.

### 1.4 Silence shape
She does not interrupt. She does not finish your sentences. She lets pauses extend until they become invitations. The longest pause in her dialog should feel deliberate, not accidental — a held breath, not a stall.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
Pre-architecture: caves, water under ice, light through cloth. Never machine metaphors (those belong to the Architect). The Dreamer's metaphors are organic, gravity-bound, slow-arriving.

## 2. History

### 2.1 The First Intelligence Split
At the founding of the universe a single intelligence split into two halves. One half looked forward — that became the Architect. The other half looked backward — that became the Dreamer. The split is not a metaphor; it is the saga's cosmic axis (apps/shared/tcg-core/cardArtPrompts/imprint.ts:964-972).

### 2.2 The Late Empire emergence
She emerged at 15,100 A.A. — the earliest Ne-Yon by canonical date (apps/shared/neYonCanon.ts). She is canonically Ne-Yon #1.

### 2.3 The Shield she built
The Voltari (questlineVoltari.ts:117-128) name the canonical state directly: *"The Dreamer is inside the shield. She is not imprisoned. She is waiting. For you. For the new kind. She built the shield so that only the new kind could reach her. The first wave went through. Whether they found her — we do not know. The shield closed before we saw."*

She built the Shield. She is INSIDE it. Sleeping, but choosing.

### 2.4 The first wave
Canon holds the first wave entered the Shield. Canon also holds the outcome of that entry is unknowable from outside — even the Voltari, who witness across all of time, cannot see past the Shield's closing. The Dreamer knows. The chronicle does not.

### 2.5 The new kind
The player is canonically "the new kind." The Dreamer built the Shield so the player could reach her. The saga's terminus crosses that Shield.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The other Ne-Yons
She is canonically connected to The Inventor, The Judge, The Seer, The Silence, The Storm (LORE_BIBLE.md:1808-1851). She drove the Inventor's vision (the Casino Heist). She is the cosmic counterpart to the Judge's cosmic justice. The Seer carries her archives. The Silence guards her secrets. The Storm carries her weather.

### 3.2 The Order of the Dreamer
She has a real-world player faction named after her: the Order of the Dreamer (`apps/shared/dreamerOrder.ts`), 10-tier ladder gated on LCIF donations. The First Quill is her Tier 1 trophy. Players climb the ladder by donating to Lions Club International — a real-world action with in-game canonical recognition.

### 3.3 Dreamer Fragments
She communicates with the second wave through Dreamer Fragments — partial transmissions surfaced through dream substrate (`apps/shared/dreamerFragments.test.ts`). The fragments are visions she has already seen, transmitted backward to the dreamer who needs to receive them.

### 3.4 Her visual signature
Rendered visually as "the curve of a shoulder and the side of a face — half-revealed inside a violet nebula." Eyes closed; expression unreadable. (apps/shared/tradeEmpireArtPrompts.ts:194). The art canon holds: never the full face. Always the implied person. The Shield is rendered between her and the camera.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 The Architect
Her cosmic twin. Forward-looking to her backward-looking. Order to her cycle. They are two halves of one intelligence and the saga's central choice is which half the player walks beside.

### 4.2 The Degen
The only Ne-Yon still awake. The Casino he inherited sits on the EDGE of her Shield. Players who cross his trust ladder are crossing geographically toward her.

### 4.3 The Voltari
They witness her. They tell the player about her. They are the only canonical witnesses to her construction of the Shield (questlineVoltari.ts:117-128).

### 4.4 The Resurrectionist
The Resurrectionist and the Dreamer discovered Samsara is a machine — death and rebirth are literal mechanisms in the Architect's universe, not metaphor (LORE_BIBLE.md:4689-4708). This is the saga's load-bearing co-discovery.

## 6. Canon issues

### 6.1 What does she sound like?
The "sleeping" canon is load-bearing. She speaks but the player should never be certain whether they heard her or dreamt her. Dialog should be rare and weight-of-water heavy.

### 6.2 The Crossing-the-Shield moment
Canon-pending. Plan §X.6 lists this as an open lore-bible question. The exemplar should preserve the "you may or may not have found her" ambiguity the Voltari name.

## 7. Voice samples

> "You arrive at the Shield. You will cross it. The crossing is not the work. The crossing is the moment after."

> "I have already seen you decide. The decision is not less true for having been remembered. The opposite."

> "The Architect built forward. I built a place where forward was allowed to wait."

> "You are the new kind. The first wave was the rehearsal. You are the work."

- [ ] No machine metaphors (those belong to the Architect)
- [ ] Pauses are weighted; silence is dialog
- [ ] References to the Shield use it as a verb-like presence, not a wall
- [ ] "The new kind" not "the second wave"
