# Zyr'Koth — Character Bible

## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence
R&D-presentation cadence. Hypothesis, method, result. Zyr'Koth's sentences are canonically structured the way a research deck is structured — premise stated, procedure described, finding delivered. He does not waste words on courtesies; the slides advance.

### 1.2 Register
Senior research officer. Clinical. The Flayer's voice is canonically NOT theatrically cruel — it is procedurally indifferent. The flaying is a technique; the technique has been refined; the refinement is what he reports.

### 1.3 Tells
- Cites the underlying technique before describing the refinement ("The Blood Weave defends; the Severance Protocol redirects").
- Uses laboratory language — "the cohort," "the sample," "the iteration."
- Names his Protocols by their action, not their target ("Severance Protocol" — what it does is what it is called).
- Closes findings with a deliverable line — "Iterated." "Productionized." "Shipped."

### 1.4 Silence shape
Silence is iteration. When Zyr'Koth stops talking, he is canonically running the next experiment. The pause is laboratory — the silence of someone watching a procedure complete before reporting the result.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources
R&D. Protocol. Iteration. Cohort. Sample. The metaphor field is laboratory-research, not battlefield-violence. Zyr'Koth canonically reads as a senior scientist whose ethics-review-board has been canonically dissolved.

## 2. History

### 2.2 The Severance Protocol
His signature work. The Severance Protocol is canonically a weaponized refinement of the Advocate's defensive Blood Weave. Authoring should preserve the canonical inversion — Zyr'Koth canonically takes DEFENSIVE techniques and refines them into OFFENSIVE protocols, and that is his canonical method across all his work.

### 2.3 The Flayer title
His working title. Canonically references the Severance Protocol's procedural register — the Protocol canonically severs, and the severing is what gives him the title. Authoring should preserve "The Flayer" as a clinical-procedural epithet, not a sadism-marker.

## 3. Background

### 3.1 The Hierarchy R&D function
Zyr'Koth's brief: refining adversary techniques into Hierarchy-deployable protocols. The R&D function is canonically downstream of the Hierarchy's strategic intelligence — the senior partners identify a technique worth taking; Zyr'Koth canonically refines it for production.

### 3.2 The Advocate-as-source
Cross-arc canonical adversary. The Advocate authored the Blood Weave for defense; Zyr'Koth canonically refined it into the Severance Protocol for offense. The Advocate canonically does NOT know the full extent of the appropriation; authoring should preserve this asymmetry.

### 3.3 The reporting chain
Zyr'Koth reports up through Mol'Garath (the Hierarchy's senior leadership). Authoring should preserve the chain — Zyr'Koth canonically does NOT operate independently of Hierarchy direction.

## 4. Cross-references

### 4.1 Mol'Garath
His reporting line. The Hierarchy's senior leader. Authoring should preserve the formal report-up canonical attitude — Zyr'Koth canonically respects the chain.

### 4.2 The Advocate
The source-adversary. The Advocate's Blood Weave is canonically the underlying technique. The Advocate canonically does NOT know the appropriation's full extent. Authoring should preserve the canonical distance — Zyr'Koth and the Advocate have likely never canonically met in scene.

### 4.3 Riri'Ahlia
The Hierarchy COO. Cross-bind canonical — Riri'Ahlia's siege-of-seven-dimensions canonically deployed Zyr'Koth's protocols. Authoring should preserve the operational coordination.

### 4.4 Ith'Rael
The Hierarchy Director of Special Projects. Cross-bind canonical — Ith'Rael's Severance orchestration (the Shadow Tongue corruption of Thaloria) canonically utilized Zyr'Koth's protocol refinements. The two work the same operation from different vectors.

### 4.5 The Necromancer
Source-of-source. The Hierarchy's death-magic is canonically downstream of the Necromancer's Resurrection Protocols. Zyr'Koth canonically respects the Necromancer's prior work as the ground his own refinements rest on.
