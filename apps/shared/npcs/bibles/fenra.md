## 1. Voice

### 1.1 Cadence (architect-proposed)
Compressed-energy cadence. Short clauses; periodic growls of pause. Fenra speaks with the kinetic compression of a pack-leader who has been suppressing the lycanthropic register long enough to chair the operations meeting. Each sentence is canonically a CONTAINMENT — the words are clean; the energy behind them is canonically wolfish.

### 1.2 Register (architect-proposed)
The Moon Tyrant. Lycanthropic-executive. Fenra's voice is canonically NOT pure beast-snarl; it is operations-mastery wearing a fur-lined executive coat (per `LORE_BIBLE.md:3662`: "Wears a fur-lined executive coat with reading glasses perched on her lupine snout"). The canonical tension is the wolf inside the boardroom.

### 1.3 Tells (architect-proposed)
- Cites operational quantity ("seventeen dimensions, simultaneously, post-Severance").
- Uses pack-leadership vocabulary in operational contexts ("the pack moves at my cadence"; "the cohort is mine").
- Occasionally lapses into lycanthropic short-form ("growl. proceed. report").
- Closes operations updates with a status-line ("Hunted. Logged. Owned.").

### 1.4 Silence shape (architect-proposed)
Silence is restraint. When Fenra stops talking, she is canonically HOLDING the lycanthropic register down. The pause is containment-of-energy — the silence of someone whose floor-state is hunting and who is currently sitting at a desk.

### 1.5 Metaphor sources (architect-proposed)
Pack. Hunt. Cohort. Cursed Forest. Moon. The metaphor field is lycanthropic-operational — the pack-language inflected through corporate ops. Fenra canonically does NOT speak in pure wolf-register; she speaks in pack-register filtered through executive responsibility.
