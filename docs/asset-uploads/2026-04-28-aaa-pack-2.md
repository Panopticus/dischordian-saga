### Producer dir → CDN segment normalisation

The producer used `dimensional/` and `elemental/` (adjective form); the
existing card definitions in `apps/shared/tcg-core/cards/definitions/`
reference `art/cards/dimension/` and `art/cards/element/` (noun form).
The upload script renames those two on the way in to keep a single
canonical CDN tree. All other producer directories pass through
unchanged.

### Tier-grid composites

Files matching `*_tier_grid.webp` (imprint) and `*_allegiance_grid.webp`
(allegiance) are 4-up review composites — useful for QA but not
referenced as per-card art. They land at
`art/cards/<category>/_grids/<file>.webp` to keep them out of the
per-card slug namespace.

## Filename ↔ cardId mapping

The producer used human-readable slugs (e.g. `cognitive_decay.webp`,
`parallax_walker.webp`) instead of the registry-canonical
`s1_<...>` cardIds in
`apps/shared/tcg-core/cardArtPrompts/`. Wiring those slugs back to the
prompt registry is a follow-up task for the cards team — these uploads
are the producer-named source of truth for that mapping pass.
