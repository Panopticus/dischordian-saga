# AAA Asset Drop — Pack 1 (2026-04-28)

Producer-delivered AAA art bundle for the Trade Empire expansion and the
S2 Hierarchy of the Damned card extension. Source archive:
`s3://dgrsart/aaa_assets_complete.zip` (1.1 GB, 225 PNGs, 194 image
files after pruning empty stub folders).

All assets converted from PNG → WebP at quality 85 (1.1 GB → 61 MB) and
uploaded under `s3://dgrsart/cdn/client-public/` with
`Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`.

Helper script: `scripts/_aaa-upload.mjs` (see flags in script header).
