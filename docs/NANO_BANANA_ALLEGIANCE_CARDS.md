# NANO BANANA — FACTION ALLEGIANCE CARDS (Phase D)

Art prompts for the 36 faction allegiance TCG cards from Phase D
(6 factions × 6 tiers each), plus 6 new "allegiance variant" card
backs that overlay the rank insignia on the existing faction back
art. Universal style anchor: dark sci-fi painterly, dramatic
chiaroscuro, 680x500px landscape art window per card.

Allegiance cards are progressively unlocked by playing + winning
matches with the matching faction. Each tier is the same character
type at a different rank — initiate → loyal servant → veteran →
victorious veteran → elite → champion. The art language should
make the rank progression visible at a glance without changing
the character's identity.

## SECTION B — ARCHITECT ALLEGIANCE (6 cards)

Tier escalation: same uniform, deeper chrome plating, more
ceremonial overlays. Tier 6 is fully ceremonial.

### Architect Initiate (Common, T1, played 10)
**Prompt**: A young Architect officer in the standard chrome-and-crimson uniform, fresh from issue, standing at attention in a featureless white intake room. The uniform is the point. Mood: not yet the badge.

### Architect Loyal Servant (Uncommon, T2, played 25)
**Prompt**: The same officer in the same uniform, posted in a doorway of an Architect surveillance hub, eyes forward, scanning a corridor that is not currently being watched by any camera. Mood: he is the camera now.

### Architect Veteran (Rare, T3, played 50)
**Prompt**: The veteran in a slightly upgraded uniform with a single chrome epaulette, standing in front of a slowly rotating hologram of a Dischordian sector map. He is the only person in the room. Mood: he has seen the inside.

### Architect Victorious Veteran (Rare, T4, won 10)
**Prompt**: The same figure with a chrome service ribbon over the heart, walking down a corridor where small surveillance drones turn to watch him pass. Mood: the Architect's quiet approval.

### Architect Elite (Epic, T5, won 50)
**Prompt**: A senior Architect in full ceremonial chrome plating standing at a desk full of unread classified memos, calmly stamping each one with the same red seal. Mood: the memos do not tell him what is going to happen, but they tell him it has been decided.

### Architect Champion (Legendary, T6, won 100)
**Prompt**: A figure in a ceremonial Architect mask with a chrome rosette badge on the chest, half-turned to face the viewer. The face is not visible. The badge is. Mood: the position, not the person.

## SECTION C — INSURGENCY ALLEGIANCE (6 cards)

Tier escalation: more scars, more salvaged gear, more tattoos.
Tier 6 is the squad's living legend.

### Insurgency Recruit (Common, T1)
**Prompt**: A young recruit in mismatched scavenged Insurgency gear, holding a rifle that is too big for them, standing in a forward base mess hall. Determined. Mood: the alternative was staying.

### Insurgency Partisan (Uncommon, T2)
**Prompt**: A partisan in a green wraparound mask sprinting across a rooftop with a satchel charge, already mid-throw. Mood: did not wait to be told.

### Insurgency Veteran (Rare, T3)
**Prompt**: A veteran in a long Insurgency duster, one visible scar across the cheek, standing in a debris field after the action. Mood: the scar shown once.

### Insurgency Victorious Veteran (Rare, T4)
**Prompt**: The same veteran with a fresh tattoo on the forearm — a small green star — sitting on the hood of a beat-up vehicle, refilling a magazine. Mood: the squad points at him.

### Insurgency Elite (Epic, T5)
**Prompt**: An Elite operator in fully customized Insurgency armor, multiple tattoos visible, a relay component on a strap across the chest. Standing in front of a downed Architect drone. Mood: first into the relay, last out.

### Insurgency Champion (Legendary, T6)
**Prompt**: A weathered Champion in a long greatcoat, standing in front of a wall covered in graffiti tags from a dozen different cells, all of them naming her. She is reading the wall. Mood: the shape you make of yourself over a hundred matches.

## SECTION D — DREAMER ALLEGIANCE (6 cards)

Tier escalation: more layers of veil, more visible third eye,
more starfield bleeding through the figure.

### Dreamer Acolyte (Common, T1)
**Prompt**: A robed acolyte with a single thin veil over the eyes, hands folded, standing in a temple antechamber lit by floating motes. Mood: still afraid of the future.

### Dreamer Visionary (Uncommon, T2)
**Prompt**: A visionary with a star-chart scroll unfurling itself in front of her, head tilted to listen. Mood: she has not yet learned to hide that she is recognizing it.

### Dreamer Veteran (Rare, T3)
**Prompt**: A Dreamer veteran with a faintly visible third eye on the forehead, holding a holographic globe of probability lines. Mood: wrong enough times to be trusted.

### Dreamer Victorious Seer (Rare, T4)
**Prompt**: The same seer with a slightly more visible third eye, looking SIDEWAYS at the viewer rather than directly. Mood: stopped being surprised. Started being concerned.

### Dreamer Elite (Epic, T5)
**Prompt**: A high-ranking Dreamer in deep indigo robes, with a starfield visible through the gaps in the robes — like the figure is a doorway, not a body. Mood: dreams the match before it happens.

### Dreamer Champion (Legendary, T6)
**Prompt**: The Dreamer herself, briefly visible: a veiled figure standing between two pillars labeled PAST and FUTURE, hands extended palms-up, the entire scene rendered with one hand in upright orientation and one hand in reversed orientation. Mood: she made herself available for one match.

## USAGE NOTES

- The 6 allegiance card backs are subtle variants of the existing
  faction backs from `docs/TCG_ART_SPEC.md`. Generate the existing
  backs first, then composite the rank insignia overlay on top.
- The tier escalation should be visually obvious within a single
  faction (Tier 1 vs Tier 6) but visually consistent across
  factions (a Tier 3 Architect and a Tier 3 Insurgent should both
  feel "rank 3" even though their identities are completely
  different).
- Champions are the faction's signature card art — extra time and
  attention there. The other 5 tiers can be generated faster.
- Faction is set in the card frame, not the art — these cards live
  in their faction's frame, and the art layers the rank
  iconography on top of the faction's visual identity.
