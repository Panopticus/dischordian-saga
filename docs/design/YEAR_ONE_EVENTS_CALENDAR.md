## 1. OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY

### The Living Universe Governance Model

Year One is not a content schedule. It is a **collective prophecy** — written by the community through action, choice, and consequence. Every week presents a new decision point. Every month a defining vote. Every season a cataclysm or revelation that can never be undone.

**Core Principles:**
- **Every vote has real impact** — not stat bonuses, but lore reveals, quest chains, pet unlocks, fighter game characters, global events, sacrifices, and permanent universe changes
- **Choices cascade** — Week 3's outcome feeds Week 4's options. Month 1's vote constrains Month 2's possibilities. The community is writing a story they cannot rewind
- **The Antiquarian records everything** — his Tome is the living history of every decision, visible to all, growing week by week
- **No artificial timers** — emergent events (Necromancer Returns, Dreamer Awakens, Terminus Advances, Shadow Tongue's Edit, Antiquarian's Revelation) still trigger from player behavior pressure, but the governance calendar provides the narrative scaffolding they wrap around
- **AI participation creates atmosphere** — simulated votes mirror real distributions, never change outcomes, and make the Governance Hub feel alive even during low-activity hours

### The Four Acts

| Act | Months | Theme | Album Resonance |
|-----|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **I: The Awakening** | 1-3 | Discovery, first stirrings, the shield flickers | *Dischordian Logic* |
| **II: The Escalation** | 4-6 | Faction wars, alliances, Fall anniversary | *The Age of Privacy* / *West By God* |
| **III: The Convergence** | 7-9 | Crises collide, corruption deepens, Terminus draws near | *The Book of Daniel 2:47* |
| **IV: The Reckoning** | 10-12 | Community-defining choices, season finale, Year Two setup | *Silence in Heaven* |

### Vote Structure

- **48 Weekly Events** — narrative beats, mini-quests, reveals, and community challenges
- **48 Weekly Votes** — one per week, 3-5 options, 7-day voting window
- **12 Monthly Defining Votes** — major choices that reshape the universe (one per month, replacing that month's final weekly vote with an elevated stakes version)
- **4 Seasonal Events** — massive multi-week events at each Act's climax
- **Daily Resource Votes** — quick binary choices for ship management (rotated from 7 templates)
- **Emergent Overlay** — living universe events trigger independently and modify whatever's happening

### 2.2 Hub Features

**Active Vote Panel** — Shows the current weekly vote with live-updating percentages. The Antiquarian's framing text sets the scene. Vote bars animate as new votes arrive (including simulated AI voters that mirror real distributions).

**Daily Choice Panel** — The binary resource allocation vote. Resets every 24 hours. Elara frames each choice as a ship management decision. Results apply immediately at reset.

**The Tome Panel** — Latest Antiquarian inscription. Tapping opens the full Tome with all historical entries. Entries unlock progressively — the Antiquarian's private annotations become visible at trust level 60+.

**Live Activity Feed** — A scrolling feed of vote activity. Mixes real player votes with AI-simulated votes (using lore-appropriate names like "Potential-4827-ARK1047"). The feed creates the feeling of a living, breathing democracy. AI votes are **never** distinguishable from real votes and **never** change outcomes.

**Your Record** — Personal voting history, streak tracking, how often you voted with the majority vs. minority. Players who consistently vote minority get unique Antiquarian commentary ("The dissenter walks a lonely corridor...").

**Community Stats** — Aggregate engagement metrics: total kills, missions, trades, arena wins, votes cast, discoveries. Updated in real-time. These numbers feed emergent event pressure meters.

**Upcoming Panel** — Previews the next major vote with countdown timer and the Antiquarian's cryptic teaser.

**History Timeline** — Scrollable timeline of all past votes. Each node shows the winning option, vote totals, and links to the Antiquarian's inscription. Players can trace how their choices cascaded through the year.

### 2.3 Ambient Life

The Hub should never feel empty. Systems that maintain the illusion of a living democracy:

- **AI Vote Drip** — Every 30-90 seconds, a simulated vote appears in the activity feed. Names are generated from the `generateVoterName()` function (Potential-XXXX-ARKXXXX format). The simulated vote distribution always mirrors the current real distribution proportionally.
- **Participation Ticker** — Running count of "Potentials active this cycle" that slowly increments.
- **NPC Commentary** — Elara, the Antiquarian, and other NPCs occasionally comment on vote trends in the feed ("Elara notes: 'The community leans toward mercy. Interesting.'").
- **Emergent Alerts** — When living universe event pressure meters are high, the Hub displays warnings: "⚠ NECROMANCER PRESSURE: 847/1000 — The dead are restless."
- **Minority Voice** — If a losing option was close (within 5%), the Antiquarian notes it: "The road not taken was nearly taken. I have recorded both paths."

## 3. THE ANTIQUARIAN'S LIVING TOME

> *"I am the Programmer. I am the Antiquarian. I collect endings. And now I inscribe the Second Coming of the Potentials — who are, I suspect, the universe's last chance to get it right."*

### 3.1 The Tome as Game Mechanic

The Antiquarian's Tome is not flavor text — it is the **canonical history of the server**. Every community decision, every vote outcome, every emergent event is inscribed by the Antiquarian in his characteristic voice: poetic, personally invested, sometimes disagreeing with the choice.

**Tome Features:**
- **Chronological History** — Every week adds a new entry. By Year's end, the Tome contains 48+ inscriptions forming a complete narrative of the community's Year One
- **The Antiquarian's Voice** — He writes as someone who has seen seventeen timelines. He knows things he shouldn't. He occasionally slips and reveals future possibilities, then crosses them out ("I should not have written that. But I did. And now you've read it.")
- **Private Annotations** — At trust level 60+, players can see the Antiquarian's margin notes. These contain his real opinions, hidden lore connections, and hints about what the community's trajectory might trigger
- **The Second Coming Chronicle** — The Tome explicitly frames the players as "the Second Coming of the Potentials" — the second wave, since the first wave has completely disappeared from the universe leaving no trace. The Antiquarian is deeply troubled by this disappearance and searches for answers in every decision the new Potentials make
- **Cross-References** — Tome entries reference each other. A Month 6 entry might say "As I wrote in Week 12 — they chose fire then, and fire has followed them since"
- **Voltari Discovery Thread** — The Antiquarian begins detecting anomalies early (Month 2) that he cannot explain. His entries about these anomalies grow increasingly agitated. By Month 8, he realizes something is communicating in electromagnetic patterns. The community's choices determine whether First Contact is peaceful or catastrophic

### 3.3 The Disappearance of the First Potentials

A running thread through the entire Tome: the Antiquarian is haunted by the fact that the first wave of Potentials — the ones who should have been here — vanished without a trace. No bodies. No records. No echoes in any of the seventeen timelines. He searches for them in every decision the second wave makes, looking for patterns, for clues, for any sign of what happened.

This thread escalates across the year:
- **Act I:** He mentions it casually. "You are the second wave. I do not know what became of the first."
- **Act II:** He becomes concerned. Patterns in the community's choices remind him of something he can't place.
- **Act III:** He finds a fragment — a single Tome entry from the first wave, written in handwriting identical to his own, that he does not remember writing. It says only: "They chose to forget."
- **Act IV:** The community votes on whether to pursue the truth about the first Potentials or let the mystery rest. This choice defines the Year Two opening.

## 4. DAILY VOTES & RESOURCE ALLOCATION

Daily votes are the heartbeat of the Governance Hub. They keep players returning, create a habit of participation, and make ship management feel collective.

### 4.1 Daily Vote Rotation

Seven templates rotate based on date (deterministic via `getDailyVote()`):

| Day Template | Question | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dream Reserves** | How should the Ark allocate today's Dream reserves? | Defense (+10% TD turret damage) | Research (+10% lore discovery rate) |
| **Signal Analysis** | A signal fragment was intercepted. Who analyzes it? | Elara — fast, surface-level | Antiquarian — slow, deep |
| **Crew Rotation** | Which department gets extra hands? | Armory (+5% fight XP) | Engineering (+5% crafting success) |
| **Power Grid** | Power fluctuation. Prioritize which system? | Shields (reduced TD core damage) | Scanners (reveal hidden items) |
| **Meme Broadcast** | The Meme broadcasts on two frequencies. Which? | Alpha (comedy, +mood) | Omega (cryptic lore, +discovery) |
| **Rations** | Feed the crew or stockpile? | Feed (+3% all XP) | Stockpile (+50 Dream banked) |
| **Specimen** | Stowaway specimen found. Release or study? | Release (+20% encounters) | Study (+15% evolution XP) |

### 4.2 Daily Vote Escalation

During major narrative moments, daily votes can escalate:
- **During Necromancer pressure >800:** "The dead whisper through the vents. Do we seal Deck 3 or send a team to investigate?"
- **During Terminus advance:** "Thought Virus detected in water recycling. Purge the system (lose 1 day progress) or quarantine and study?"
- **During Shadow Tongue corruption:** "Archive text is changing. Lock the Archives (no lore access for 12h) or let the Antiquarian attempt a purification ritual?"
