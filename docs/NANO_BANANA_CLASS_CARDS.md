# NANO BANANA — CLASS CARDS (Phase B)

Art prompts for the 30 player-class TCG cards authored in Phase B,
plus the 6 new card backs (one per player class).

## Universal style anchor

**Append to every prompt below**:
*"Dark sci-fi painterly, Duelyst meets Blade Runner meets cosmic
horror, dramatic chiaroscuro, rich textures, 680x500px landscape
card art window."*

Class cards live INSIDE the existing TCG card frame system. The
art style matches the rest of the Dischordian set; what's new is
the per-class color palette and iconographic language.

## SECTION A — CARD BACKS (6 new, 750x1050px each)

### `card_back_class_spy.png` — Spy
**Palette**: Slate (#1e293b), signal cyan (#22d3ee), encrypted black, scope-red dot
**Prompt**: A surveillance camera ring with one camera deliberately blacked out. Inside the dark camera, a single red dot. Schematic spy-craft glyphs around the ring. Mood: somebody is in the room and the room does not yet know it.

### `card_back_class_oracle.png` — Oracle
**Palette**: Veiled indigo (#4338ca), prophetic gold (#fbbf24), star-chart silver
**Prompt**: A veil-pattern of overlapping translucent eye shapes on indigo, with a single gold eye at the center looking back at the viewer. Star-chart constellations radiate outward in silver. Mood: she has been watching you read this card.

### `card_back_class_assassin.png` — Assassin
**Palette**: Glass-blade black (#0f172a), kill-red (#dc2626), bone white
**Prompt**: A single shattered glass blade caught mid-arc on a black field, fragments suspended in the air. Bone-white knife notations along the border. Mood: the strike has already happened in another timeline.

### `card_back_class_engineer.png` — Engineer
**Palette**: Brass (#b45309), blueprint cyan (#0ea5e9), solder silver, workshop warm
**Prompt**: Brass clockwork gears in a blueprint-cyan grid behind, with a wrench and a soldering iron crossed at the center. A single floating equation in the upper right that resolves itself. Mood: I built the room you are standing in.

### `card_back_class_soldier.png` — Soldier
**Palette**: Iron grey (#475569), insurgency green (#22c55e), formation gold
**Prompt**: A formation diagram — five chevrons in iron grey on a darker grey field, with a green tactical arrow cutting through them. Rivets at the four corners. Mood: I will be here when you arrive.

### `card_back_class_neyon.png` — Ne-Yon (meta-class)
**Palette**: Hybrid violet (#7c3aed), discipline gold (#fbbf24), syncretic teal (#14b8a6)
**Prompt**: Five overlapping circles in five different colors (one per other class), arranged in a quincunx, with the center where they all overlap painted in a sixth color (Ne-Yon hybrid violet). The pattern reads as both a diagram and a target. Mood: I am all five at once and none of them perfectly.

## SECTION B — SPY CLASS (5 cards)

### `s1_class_spy_01_signal_ghost.webp` — Signal Ghost (Common)
**Prompt**: A figure in a long coat dissolving into a wall of static at the edge of a Babylonian alleyway. Half-visible. Insurgency green signal-trace marks behind them. Mood: gone before the camera blink.

### `s1_class_spy_02_dead_drop.webp` — Dead Drop (Uncommon spell)
**Prompt**: A gloved hand placing a small folded paper into a hollow brick on a rain-slick wall. The brick is the only thing in focus. The rain is in focus too, somehow. Mood: this address is not on any map.

### `s1_class_spy_03_cover_name.webp` — Cover Name (Rare)
**Prompt**: A spy looking at her own reflection in a shop window. The reflection has a different face. She is not surprised. Mood: I built her last week and she has my appointments today.

### `s1_class_spy_04_burn_the_handler.webp` — Burn the Handler (Epic spell)
**Prompt**: An open envelope on fire on a desk, the burning paper revealing fragments of a coded message. A second hand setting it down and walking away. No face. Mood: every operative kills the person who recruited them.

### `s1_class_spy_05_twelfth_archons_apprentice.webp` — The Twelfth Archon's Apprentice (Legendary)
**Prompt**: A young woman in The Human's old long coat, standing in an Insurgency safehouse with a case file open on the table. The case file's pages are partially black-marker-redacted. She is reading the redactions, not the text. Mood: the apprentice he chose is the one nobody saw him choose.

## SECTION C — ORACLE CLASS (5 cards)

### `s1_class_oracle_01_auspex.webp` — Auspex (Common)
**Prompt**: A pale bird with an iris-colored eye perched on a star chart, head tilted to look directly at the viewer. The eye is too aware. Mood: it knows where the body is.

### `s1_class_oracle_02_prescient_glyph.webp` — Prescient Glyph (Uncommon spell)
**Prompt**: A glowing geometric glyph carved into stone, hovering in mid-air. The glyph is mid-rotation. Behind it, a chronometer that has stopped. Mood: drawn yesterday for today's emergency.

### `s1_class_oracle_03_reader_of_tomorrows.webp` — Reader of Tomorrows (Rare)
**Prompt**: A robed figure with a holographic scroll unfurled in front of her. The scroll displays not text but a turning probability cloud. Her free hand is reaching INTO the scroll. Mood: she is correcting a future from inside it.

### `s1_class_oracle_04_second_sight.webp` — Second Sight (Epic spell)
**Prompt**: A pair of overlaid eyes drawn in gold leaf — one open, one closed — superimposed on a starfield. The closed eye is the one that's actually seeing. Mood: the room's opinion of you is showing.

### `s1_class_oracle_05_oracles_unbroken_signal.webp` — The Oracle's Unbroken Signal (Legendary)
**Prompt**: A holographic projection of the White Oracle's veiled silhouette, rendered as scan-line glitch, broadcast from a cracked signal lattice. Her hand is extended through the broken lattice. Mood: still transmitting.

## SECTION D — ASSASSIN CLASS (5 cards)

### `s1_class_assassin_01_glass_blade_initiate.webp` — Glass Blade Initiate (Common)
**Prompt**: A young initiate in a black training suit holding a glass blade up to the dawn light. Half the blade is already cracked. Her expression is serene. Mood: she will only need it once.

### `s1_class_assassin_02_silent_step.webp` — Silent Step (Uncommon spell)
**Prompt**: A pair of footprints in damp concrete, advancing. The footprints stop at the edge of a closed door. The shadow under the door is the only suggestion of who was there. Mood: the apology is not for you.

### `s1_class_assassin_03_witness_remover.webp` — Witness Remover (Rare)
**Prompt**: An assassin in a high collar standing in a Babylonian courtyard at twilight, two short blades crossed in front of her. Her shadow is split in two. Mood: there is no math here that includes leaving anyone behind.

### `s1_class_assassin_04_execute_protocol.webp` — Execute Protocol (Epic spell)
**Prompt**: A page of redacted text — almost entirely black bars — with a single uncrossed sentence at the bottom in red. The sentence is unreadable but you can feel the shape of it. Mood: the Protocol speaks itself.

### `s1_class_assassin_05_akai_shi_first_apprentice.webp` — Akai Shi's First Apprentice (Legendary)
**Prompt**: A woman in a red Architect-style coat, mid-leap from a rooftop, two blades trailing red light. Her face is hidden by her own forward motion. The city below is unaware. Mood: nine years of silence and a ninth blade in the air.

## SECTION E — ENGINEER CLASS (5 cards)

### `s1_class_engineer_01_workshop_drone.webp` — Workshop Drone (Common)
**Prompt**: A small brass-and-cyan automaton on a workbench, holding a wrench in one manipulator and a card in the other. The card is the next card the player will draw. Mood: smaller version of the Engineer, same goggles.

### `s1_class_engineer_02_field_modification.webp` — Field Modification (Uncommon spell)
**Prompt**: A hand-soldering session lit by sparks. The component being soldered is glowing too brightly to identify. Schematic notes scrawled in chalk on the workbench. Mood: this fix was not in the manual because the manual has not been written yet.

### `s1_class_engineer_03_kinetic_containment_sink.webp` — Kinetic Containment Sink (Rare structure)
**Prompt**: A massive cylindrical containment chamber with three glowing rings, embedded in a lab wall. Inside, what looks like a frozen explosion. The walls have patches that look freshly applied. Mood: the Engineer's first Rush prototype lives here.

### `s1_class_engineer_04_prototype_blueprint.webp` — Prototype Blueprint (Epic spell)
**Prompt**: A blueprint sheet unrolling itself across an empty workbench, the lines drawing themselves in real time. A drafting compass and a Moog patch cable on the corner. Mood: the design is talking back.

### `s1_class_engineer_05_engineers_apprentice.webp` — The Engineer's Apprentice (Legendary)
**Prompt**: A young apprentice in oversized goggles seated at a workbench identical to the Engineer's, building an instrument that doesn't quite resemble what their master built. Behind them, the Engineer stands in shadow, watching, not interfering. Mood: the only way to learn is to do it differently.

## SECTION F — SOLDIER CLASS (5 cards)

### `s1_class_soldier_01_line_recruit.webp` — Line Recruit (Common)
**Prompt**: A soldier in basic Insurgency kit standing at parade-rest in a training yard at dawn. No special features. The honesty of the pose is the entire image. Mood: 3/3 for two cost.

### `s1_class_soldier_02_shieldwall.webp` — Shieldwall (Uncommon)
**Prompt**: A heavily armored figure planting a tower shield into the mud of a forward position, wedging it with a boot. Behind the shield, the rest of the formation is forming up. Mood: the formation knows the deal.

### `s1_class_soldier_03_rally_the_line.webp` — Rally the Line (Rare spell)
**Prompt**: A commander mid-sentence in a trench, sword raised, mouth open in a single shouted word. The word is illegible but the soldiers behind her have already started running forward. Mood: a rally is the sentence the room cannot leave.

### `s1_class_soldier_04_iron_vanguard.webp` — Iron Vanguard (Epic)
**Prompt**: A heavy infantry trooper in dented iron-grey armor, rushing forward at a flat sprint with a boarding axe over one shoulder. The momentum is the painting. Mood: the doctrine fits in one word.

### `s1_class_soldier_05_last_regiment_standing.webp` — The Last Regiment Standing (Legendary)
**Prompt**: A small cluster of veteran soldiers standing in a ruined plaza after the war is officially over, weapons still up, looking outward at threats nobody has told them are gone. The plaza is empty. The threats are still there for them. Mood: nobody told them.

## USAGE NOTES

- The 6 card backs are the priority — generate those first so the
  per-card art has a frame context to land in.
- The Ne-Yon meta-class back is the trickiest because it has to
  reference all five other class palettes simultaneously without
  reading as a logo.
- Spy / Oracle / Assassin cards lean toward in-shadow framings;
  Engineer / Soldier / Ne-Yon lean toward direct illumination.
- Faction is set in the card frame, not the art — these cards live
  in Insurgency / Architect / Antiquarian / Neutral frames depending
  on the per-card data, but the art identity is class-first.
