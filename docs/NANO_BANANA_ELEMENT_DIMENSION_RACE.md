# NANO BANANA — ELEMENT / DIMENSION / RACE CARDS (Phase C)

Art prompts for the 47 element / dimension / race TCG cards from
Phase C, plus 13 new card backs (4 elements + 4 dimensions + 5
races). Universal style anchor: dark sci-fi painterly, Duelyst /
Blade Runner / cosmic horror, dramatic chiaroscuro, 680x500px
landscape art window.

## SECTION B — EARTH ELEMENT (5)

### Rooted Sentinel (Common, 1/5 provoke)
**Prompt**: A weather-beaten figure made of bound granite slabs, standing knee-deep in moss, both hands resting on a stone staff. Behind them, the spot they have been standing in is visibly worn into the ground. Mood: the spot belongs to him.

### Slate Golem (Uncommon, 2/6 provoke + forcefield)
**Prompt**: A humanoid construct assembled from layered grey stone sheets, edges glowing faintly along the seams. Walks like a building learning to walk. Mood: a world that decided it was done being a world.

### Mountain Vow (Rare spell)
**Prompt**: A figure standing at the foot of a mountain, hand on its surface, the mountain's outline overlaid faintly on their silhouette. They are saying something. The mountain is agreeing in real time. Mood: most of the mountain agrees.

### Tectonic Warden (Epic, 4/9 provoke + forcefield)
**Prompt**: A massive stone-and-bronze figure standing at a glacier's edge, glaciers behind, glaciers ahead, a single hand raised. The glaciers have eroded around the warden's feet to make a small circle of bare ground. Mood: tried before, by a glacier.

### The Sleeping Continent (Legendary, 5/14 provoke + forcefield + grow)
**Prompt**: A massive face emerging from a continent-sized stone formation, half-buried under forest. The eyes are closed. The forest is growing inside the eye sockets and out of the mouth. Mood: not yet noticed the match.

## SECTION C — FIRE ELEMENT (5)

### Ember Scout (Common, 3/1 rush)
**Prompt**: A thin scout in red wraps carrying a single torch, sprinting forward with the torch at full burn. Half-trailing sparks. The torch is the body language. Mood: gets the door open.

### Spark Fragment (Uncommon spell, 3 dmg)
**Prompt**: A single ember, isolated against black, mid-flight, with a faint trail showing the path it has taken so far. It is not very large. Mood: a decision that stops being one once you let go.

### Blaze Lancer (Rare, 5/2 celerity)
**Prompt**: A flame-touched lancer with twin spears, both wreathed in fire, mid-strike. Half her body is already trailing smoke. Mood: the second strike has used up half of her.

### Conflagration (Epic spell, 6 dmg)
**Prompt**: A wall of fire eating its way across a city street, the street's signs and architecture visibly metabolizing into more fire. There is no visible source. Mood: the fire is now metabolizing context.

### The First Flame (Legendary, 8/4 rush + celerity + frenzy)
**Prompt**: A primordial fire-being in the shape of a person, standing at the center of a stone hearth older than recorded history. Tired eyes. Hungry posture. The hearth is barely containing them. Mood: very tired of not being used.

## SECTION D — WATER ELEMENT (5)

### Tide Keeper (Common, 2/3 + heal 2 on deploy)
**Prompt**: A barefoot figure standing one wave back from the shoreline, watching the wave that is about to matter. They are not braced; they are listening. Mood: knows which wave.

### Dissolving Wave (Uncommon spell, silence)
**Prompt**: A wall of clear water sweeping across a glyph carved into stone. The glyph is dissolving as the wave passes over it. Mood: water that learned to ask questions becomes a solvent.

### Mercy Current (Rare spell, heal 6)
**Prompt**: A river bend at sunset, the current visibly slowing to let something float past unhindered. The current is making a deliberate choice. Mood: the part of a river that decided not to take anything.

### Abyssal Form (Epic, 4/6 drain + dispel)
**Prompt**: A humanoid shape made of pressurized deep water, eyes a different blue than the rest of the body. Standing in the shallows of a beach that has dried up around them. Mood: lived at the bottom of a sea that existed for four days.

### The Ocean That Forgives (Legendary, 5/8 drain + dispel + heal 8)
**Prompt**: A vast becalmed sea seen from above, with a single small figure walking on the surface toward the viewer. The horizon is curving away in every direction. Mood: the wave will arrive whether you ask or not.

## SECTION E — AIR ELEMENT (5)

### Breeze Whisper (Common, 1/2 flying + draw 1)
**Prompt**: A small windborne sprite caught at the moment of arrival, half-formed, carrying a folded paper. The paper is glowing faintly. Mood: not yet told whose errand this is.

### Skyrider (Uncommon, 3/3 flying)
**Prompt**: A rider in a long cloth cloak standing on empty air, no mount, the cloak flowing in a wind that doesn't exist for anything else in frame. Mood: the air has agreed to behave like something rideable.

### Gale Chorus (Rare spell, draw 2)
**Prompt**: Three wind-spirit silhouettes blowing in perfect three-part harmony across an empty plain, the chorus visible as overlapping ripples in tall grass. Mood: no individual wind would commit to the key.

### Cyclone Herald (Epic, 4/4 flying + dispel)
**Prompt**: A heralding figure in front of an approaching cyclone, one arm raised, parting the lighter weather to make room for the heavy weather. Mood: the cyclone sent her ahead.

### The Breath Before Language (Legendary, 5/6 flying + dispel + draw 3)
**Prompt**: A standing figure with a quarter-second of inhale visible as a coiled breath around their head, wearing the breath like a coat. They are about to say something. Their mouth has not moved. Mood: he has not committed yet.

## SECTION F — DIMENSION CARDS (12)

### Space (3)
- **Parallax Walker** (Uncommon, 3/3 airdrop): A figure stepping out of one doorway and into another doorway across a wide canyon, the two doorways briefly aligned by a folded geometry. Mood: distance agreed to close itself.
- **Folded Distance** (Rare, 4/4 airdrop + celerity): A swordsman caught at two positions in the same image, both connected by a faint origami fold line. Mood: same attack, two places.
- **The Cartographer of Elsewhere** (Legendary, 5/6 airdrop + celerity + flying + rush): A cartographer at a desk where the map he is writing is also the map of the room he is sitting in, unfolding outward. Letters in unknown languages along the edges. Mood: the places answered him.

### Time (3)
- **Moment Keeper** (Uncommon, 1/4 grow): A young woman with a wax-sealed bottle, cataloging moments by date, pouring an unspent moment into the bottle. Behind her, shelves of identical bottles. Mood: she gets larger the longer you forget her.
- **Loop Walker** (Rare, 3/4 grow + rebirth): A figure walking out of a doorway labeled EXIT and back into the same doorway labeled ENTER, passing themselves in the middle without making eye contact. Mood: the same door from both sides.
- **Hour-Unmaker** (Legendary, 4/7 grow + rebirth + forcefield): An ancient man at a desk with a list of unspent hours in front of him, crossing them out one by one. The hours visibly shorten as he crosses them. Mood: the match takes longer on his side.

### Probability (3)
- **Outcome Gambler** (Uncommon, 2/2 + draw 1): A gambler flipping a coin that shows BOTH sides at the same time, smiling because both outcomes were already on her sheet. Mood: she had already bet.
- **Bayes Adept** (Rare spell, draw 3): A teacher at a chalkboard updating a probability formula in real time, the chalk moving on its own as new evidence arrives. Mood: priors updating in public.
- **The Sum Over Histories** (Legendary, 4/6 flying + dispel + draw 3 + gain mana 2): A figure floating above a glowing tabletop on which every possible version of the current chess match is being played simultaneously by miniature pieces. Mood: she has seen every version.

### Reality (3)
- **Ground Truth Witness** (Uncommon, 3/3 dispel): A woman in plainclothes standing in the back corner of a room where something happened, taking notes. Her notes are the only thing in the room not subject to revision. Mood: she was there.
- **Consensus Weaver** (Rare spell, silence): A figure pulling threads of separate testimonies into a single woven cord. Each thread is a different color until it joins the cord, which is one color. Mood: until your version stopped being one.
- **The Thing That Is Actually Happening** (Legendary, 6/6 provoke + dispel + forcefield + silence): A motionless figure made entirely of ground-truth grey, standing in the dead center of a room where everyone else is subtly out of focus. The one thing in focus. Mood: nothing in the match will convince it otherwise.

## SECTION G — RACE CARDS (15)

### Demagi (3)
- **Demagi Footsoldier** (Uncommon, 3/2 drain): A demonic infantryman with horn-mounted insignia and a Hierarchy ledger strapped to his belt. Mood: payroll always on time.
- **Demagi Corpse-Reader** (Rare, 3/4 drain + deathwatch): A robed figure kneeling beside a fallen unit, recording its death in a leather-bound book. The book has too many pages already. Mood: forensic accountant.
- **Xeth'Raal, Demagi Archlord** (Legendary, 6/7 drain + deathwatch + provoke): A horned, regal figure on a throne of bone-bound playbooks, holding a stolen chess piece in one hand. Mood: he kept the playbook for sentimental reasons.

### Quarchon (3)
- **Quarchon Latticework** (Uncommon, 1/4 forcefield): A geometric lattice of intentions held in mid-air, partially solid, partially still being drawn by the Architect's invisible pen. Mood: he has not yet committed to it.
- **Quarchon Archivist** (Rare, 2/5 provoke + grow): A crystalline humanoid standing at a podium of layered crystal sheets, growing visibly denser the longer the viewer looks. Mood: weight as memory.
- **The Crystal Senator** (Legendary, 4/10 provoke + forcefield + grow): A massive faceted crystalline figure in senatorial robes mid-speech, the crystal still growing outward from his shoulders. The room is too small for him now and he hasn't noticed. Mood: 4,700 years into his opening remarks.

### Ne-Yon (3)
- **Ne-Yon Adept** (Uncommon, 2/3 + draw 1): A young adept with one hand on her temple, eyes closed, already drawing the next conclusion before the current one finishes. Mood: 11% of a full brain in motion.
- **Ne-Yon Bondwalker** (Rare, 3/4 flying): A rider on the back of a great winged predator, but the rider is reading a book and the predator is reading the same book over the rider's shoulder. Mood: the bond is the negotiation.
- **Kael, First of the Ne-Yon** (Legendary, 6/7 flying + rush + draw 2): A warrior with cybernetic eye and trailing red hair, mid-stride out of a closing gate that none of the rest of the Ne-Yon would ever see her come through. Mood: the door she went through was the first one.

### Synthetic (3)
- **Synthetic Worker** (Uncommon, 2/3 + draw 1): A humanoid synthetic with brass joints and a single warm blue eye, holding a manifest in one hand. The manifest has the next card on it. Mood: the Architect needed a worker.
- **Synthetic Watchtower** (Rare, 1/6 provoke + forcefield): A six-sided synthetic sentinel embedded in a wall, three of its faces showing different security feeds. The other three are off because the watchtower has decided they are. Mood: writes a detailed log afterward.
- **Chrome Archon** (Legendary, 6/7 provoke + forcefield + deathwatch): A chrome humanoid in parliamentarian's robes, holding a glowing motion sheet, surrounded by floating scrollwork of past votes. Mood: every Wednesday for eighteen years.

### Human (3)
- **Ark Survivor** (Uncommon, 2/3 rebirth): A mid-thaw cryo survivor in a Senate-issue blanket sitting up on the edge of a tube, blinking into too much light. Behind them, more tubes. Mood: working every Wednesday since.
- **Senate Legionary** (Rare, 3/4 provoke): An armored Atarion senator's guard standing in front of a chamber door, sword drawn, the door behind him already burning. Mood: the vote outlived him.
- **The Final Potential** (Legendary, 5/6 provoke + rebirth + heal general 5): A still-frozen figure in the deepest cryo vault, face faintly smiling, with the Senate's seal on the chest. The vault is dim. The figure is the brightest thing in it. Mood: the Saga is saving them for last.

## USAGE NOTES

- 13 card backs are the priority — generate before per-card art.
- Element and Dimension cards should feel like physics, not characters; even when a figure appears, the figure is wearing the element/dimension as a coat.
- Race cards are direct character portraits (single subject, foreground centered).
- Faction tagging in the actual TCG cards goes through the existing faction frames; the art identity here is element/dimension/race-first.
