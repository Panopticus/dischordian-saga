## Shot 01 (0:00–0:15) — Black with breath

**START FRAME:**
Pure black. No subject. A single faint cyan vector line traces a heartbeat across the lower third of the frame, flickering on and off like a dying telemetry feed. Tiny white serif text bottom-left: "ARK 1047 — TELEMETRY RECOVERED — CYCLE 93,847." Anamorphic lens flare ghosts at frame edges. 8K hyper-realistic, total darkness with one light source. Film grain heavy.

**END FRAME:**
The cyan heartbeat has thickened into a steady rhythm and a second line — rose, slightly desynced — has joined it from the right edge. The serif text has dissolved. A pinpoint of warm white light has appeared dead-center, the size of a needle's head. The black field is no longer pure: faint volumetric fog drifts across the frame from below, lit by the new pinpoint. 8K hyper-realistic.

**MOTION (0:00→0:15):**
Camera is locked. Cyan line stabilizes; rose line joins; pinpoint of light slowly sharpens into being.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 02 start-frame — the white pinpoint becomes the cryo-pod interior light source.

## Shot 02 (0:15–0:30) — The pod

**START FRAME:**
Extreme close-up on a frosted oval window of a cryo pod, viewed from outside. Through the frost: the suggestion of a face — closed eyelids, lashes rimed with crystalline ice, lips slightly parted. The pinpoint white from Shot 01 is now revealed as a single LED above the window, pulsing once per second. Cold cyan ambient bleeds through the ice from interior pod-lights. The frost pattern on the glass is fractal and exquisitely detailed. Macro lens, 8K hyper-realistic, anamorphic flare from the LED.

**END FRAME:**
The frost on the glass has begun to recede in spreading veins, melting from the LED outward. The eyelids beneath are still closed but the lashes have shed their ice. A second pulse of the LED — stronger now, with a faint amber undertone replacing the cold cyan. Visible breath fog briefly clouds the inside of the glass. Same macro framing.

**MOTION (0:15→0:30):**
Frost recedes radially. LED intensifies and warms. Camera holds; the only motion is melt and breath.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 03 start-frame — pull back from the pod window as the pod itself comes into view.

## Shot 03 (0:30–0:45) — Pod row reveal

**START FRAME:**
Camera has pulled back to mid-shot. We now see the full cryo pod — a tall sarcophagus of brushed dark titanium and reinforced glass — and the player-character barely visible behind the still-frosting glass. Around the pod, in symmetric rows stretching into the depth of frame, are dozens of identical pods. All other pods are dark. Only ours has the pulsing amber LED. The chamber is enormous — vaulted ceilings, ribbed structural arches, dust motes floating in volumetric god-rays from a single overhead emergency strobe far above. Wide lens, low angle looking up the pod row. 8K hyper-realistic, deep blacks, cold cyan ambient from dormant pods, single warm spot on ours.

**END FRAME:**
Same wide shot, same low angle — but a slow ripple of amber LEDs has begun to pulse down the row, one pod at a time, like a Mexican wave of awakening. Six pods in either direction now glow warm. The overhead strobe has stabilized into a steady spot. Dust motes catch the new light and form ribbons. Visible in the deep background: a narrow corridor leading away, with the ghost of a holographic figure standing at its threshold — too small to read clearly, but unmistakably there. 8K hyper-realistic.

**MOTION (0:30→0:45):**
Camera holds. Light wave propagates down the row. Hologram materializes at the corridor mouth.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 04 start-frame — push in toward the holographic figure at the corridor mouth.

## Shot 04 (0:45–1:00) — Elara, dormant

**START FRAME:**
Camera has pushed forward through the pod row. We are now near the corridor threshold. The holographic figure resolves: **Elara**, life-size, a young woman with pale skin and short cyan-glowing hair, dressed in simple ship-issue tunic, eyes closed, arms at her sides, slightly translucent. Her form flickers — every few seconds a frame of static distorts her silhouette. She stands inside a faint cyan projection cylinder rising from a floor emitter. Behind her: the corridor, dark, lined with running-light strips that are dim but not out. Medium shot, eye-level, 8K hyper-realistic, the cyan of her hair the dominant accent color in an otherwise cold-grey corridor.

**END FRAME:**
Same medium shot. Elara's eyes have opened — large, gentle, glowing softly with warm internal cyan light, not blue-cold but lit-from-within. Her flicker has stabilized into a clean projection. She has tilted her head a fraction, as if registering the camera (the player) for the first time. Her lips part to begin a word. Behind her, the corridor running-lights have brightened. Faint subtitle-style text begins to ghost-form in the lower frame: "ELARA — Witness Construct, Iteration 12,401."

**MOTION (0:45→1:00):**
Camera holds. Elara wakes. Her flicker stabilizes. Subtitle ghosts in.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 05 start-frame — Elara's open eyes carry into the next shot's reverse angle.

## Shot 05 (1:00–1:15) — POV from cryo

**START FRAME:**
Reverse angle: now the camera is *inside* the cryo pod, looking out through the half-melted frost. The pod glass occupies the entire foreground, a soft frosted oval lens. Beyond it, distorted by the glass and the residual ice, we see Elara — slightly defocused, her cyan light bleeding into rainbow chromatic aberration through the frost crystals. Vignette is heavy. We see what the player's character sees in the moment of first consciousness. 8K hyper-realistic, extreme shallow DoF on the frost pattern in the immediate foreground, Elara softly out of focus beyond.

**END FRAME:**
The frost has continued to melt, opening a clearer aperture in the upper-center of the glass. Through this aperture Elara is now sharp — and she is looking *directly at us*, smiling faintly. Beyond her, the corridor is lit. Beyond the corridor, far in the depth of field, an additional glow has appeared — rose-pink, distant, half-hidden behind a structural arch. (The Human's signal, first manifestation.) Same POV framing.

**MOTION (1:00→1:15):**
Frost melts. Elara comes into focus. Distant rose glow appears.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 06 start-frame — push out through the dissolving frost into clean corridor space.

## Shot 06 (1:15–1:30) — The corridor

**START FRAME:**
Wide three-quarter shot. The cryo pod door has hissed open and we see the player-character from behind, mid-thigh up — a backlit silhouette stepping out of the pod into the corridor. Their breath fogs. They are wearing only the thin grey ship undersuit, frost dusting their shoulders. Elara hovers a half-step ahead, turned to lead, her cyan projection cylinder having reformed around her boots so she appears to walk on light. Lining the corridor walls: floor-to-ceiling glass cabinets containing mechanical artifacts — a burnt card sealed in stasis, a damaged helmet, a single child's shoe, a folded letter, a steel ring. Each cabinet has a small brass plaque too small to read. Wide lens, eye-level, 8K hyper-realistic, cold corridor cyan with our protagonist a warm-grey silhouette.

**END FRAME:**
The protagonist has taken three steps forward and now stands beside Elara, profile to camera. The plaques on the cabinets have lit one by one as we passed them — a chain of small amber lights down the corridor's length, like reading lamps in a library waking up. The far end of the corridor is now visible: a circular blast door, sealed, with a faint rose pulse glowing through its seam. The player's posture is unsteady but upright — first standing in 93,847 cycles. Same wide framing.

**MOTION (1:15→1:30):**
Player steps out, walks three paces. Cabinet lights ignite in sequence. Rose glow at far door pulses once.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 07 start-frame — push down corridor toward the rose-pulsing door.

The brass plaque reads "EXHIBIT 01 — ARTIFACT OF UNKNOWN ORIGIN. DO NOT REINTRODUCE." In soft focus behind: more cases, more plaques, more artifacts. Side-profile of the player and Elara walking in slow march, both looking at the cases. Tracking dolly, eye-level, 8K hyper-realistic, museum-gallery warm pools of light against cold corridor cyan.

**END FRAME:**
The tracking has moved us past three more cases. Player has stopped. Elara has stopped beside them. The player's hand is half-raised toward the burnt card cabinet, mid-recognition. Same tracking framing held still.

**MOTION (1:30→1:45):**

## Shot 08 (1:45–2:00) — The burnt card

**START FRAME:**
Extreme close-up on the burnt card inside its vacuum vitrine. The card is canted at a 15-degree angle on a soft black velvet bed. The eye glyph is visible — a single open eye with a vertical pupil, surrounded by curling soot and the ghost of an unreadable name in faded gilt. The plaque is in the lower frame, slightly out of focus, but the words "DO NOT REINTRODUCE" are sharp. The vitrine glass reflects a faint cyan cast from Elara's projection just outside the frame. Macro lens, 8K hyper-realistic, the card occupying 80% of the frame, every charred fiber rendered.

**END FRAME:**
The eye glyph on the card has *blinked*. (Subtle — one frame of closure and reopening, captured at the end of the shot.) The pupil now appears to track the camera. The vacuum vitrine has developed a single hairline crack across its top, glowing faintly cyan along its edge. The plaque text has not changed but a small additional warning glyph has appeared beneath it: a Loredex codex symbol, the universal "this is now indexed" mark. Same macro framing.

**MOTION (1:45→2:00):**
Card holds. At 0:13 the eye blinks. Vitrine cracks. Loredex glyph appears.

**CONTINUITY:**

## Shot 09 (2:00–2:15) — The reaction

**START FRAME:**
Two-shot, eye-level, just past the vitrine. Player on left in three-quarter profile, Elara on right in three-quarter profile, both looking at the burnt card we just left. Elara's expression: knowing, unsurprised, gently sad — she has seen this before. Player's expression: not yet comprehending, but the first crack of memory is showing in their eyes. The cabinet's amber spot lights both faces from below; the corridor's cyan lights them from behind. Faces are caught between two color temperatures, halved. 8K hyper-realistic, shallow DoF on faces with the cabinet softly out of focus between them.

**END FRAME:**
Player has turned their head a fraction toward Elara. Elara has not turned but has spoken — her lips have just closed on a final syllable. Subtitle text ghost-forms beneath her: *"You are not the first. You are also not the last. You are the one who chose to wake."* The corridor's distant rose pulse has brightened, throwing rose into the right side of the frame, so Elara is now lit cyan-from-back, amber-from-below, rose-from-far. The player is lit the same way. They are bathed in all three palette colors at once. Same two-shot framing.

**MOTION (2:00→2:15):**
Camera holds. Elara speaks. Player turns. Rose distant pulse brightens.

**CONTINUITY:**

## Shot 10 (2:15–2:30) — The far door

**START FRAME:**
Camera is now low and pushing forward at floor level, racing toward the circular blast door at the corridor's end. The door is huge — three meters across, dogged with eight massive bolts, surface bearing a deep-cut emblem of the Two Witnesses (a stylized circle split by a vertical bar, half cyan half rose). The seam of the door is glowing rose-pink, brighter than before, as if something on the other side is pressing against it. Steam vents along the corridor floor near the door hiss in time with the rose pulse. Player and Elara are out of frame behind us. Low angle wide, 8K hyper-realistic, rapid forward dolly motion blur on the corridor walls, the door sharp in the center.

**END FRAME:**
The camera has reached the door. The eight bolts have all rotated open in unison. The seam is now blazing rose, the entire door rim a halo. The Two Witnesses emblem at the door's center has fissured down its vertical bar, splitting cleanly into a cyan half and a rose half that have begun to slide apart. We see only a sliver of what is beyond: a deep black void with a single rose-pink eye, far away, opening. Low angle, frame frozen at the moment of fracture.

**MOTION (2:15→2:30):**
Forward dolly the full length of the corridor. Bolts rotate. Door begins to split.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 11 start-frame — push through the opening door into the black space beyond.

## Shot 11 (2:30–2:45) — The Human, far signal

**START FRAME:**
Camera has crossed the threshold. We are now in absolute black space — no walls, no floor, no ceiling, just void. Far in the distance, a single rose-pink eye floats, unblinking, the size of a moon at this distance. Around it, faint rose-tinted pinpoints suggest a vast network or constellation. Closer to us, mid-frame, a small figure has appeared — silhouette only, backlit by the distant eye, a figure in a long coat with the brim of a hat low. **The Human.** No facial features visible, just outline, hands in pockets, posture relaxed. The void between us and the figure has texture — a faint grid like a substrate layer barely visible, breathing. Wide lens, frame fully composed, 8K hyper-realistic, almost monochrome rose-on-black.

**END FRAME:**
The Human silhouette has tipped their hat brim back slightly with one finger. We now see the lower half of their face — a faint smile, a hint of warm skin, no other detail. The distant rose eye behind them has narrowed to a horizontal slit and reopened, like a wink. The substrate grid in the void between us has briefly lit up, revealing it is a *network of voices* — faint translucent faces and figures all whispering, all aware of us. Above the Human, a single line of subtitle text materializes: *"Hey, kid. Long time. We have a lot to talk about."* Same wide composition.

**MOTION (2:30→2:45):**
Camera holds. Human tips hat. Distant eye winks. Substrate flashes its hidden network.

**CONTINUITY:**
end-frame ≈ Shot 12 start-frame — pull back fast through corridor and pod row to a final wide reveal.

## Shot 12 (2:45–3:00) — The witness

**START FRAME:**
Whip pull-back. We blast back through the doorway, down the corridor (cabinets streaking past), through the pod row (LED wave still rippling), out beyond the cryo chamber's ribbed arches, until we arrive at an extreme wide exterior shot of **Ark 1047** — a vast cylindrical generation ark drifting in deep space, hull pitted with micro-impacts, lit only by a distant binary star system. Tens of thousands of pod-bay lights speckle its surface, almost all dark. One small constellation of lights, near the bow, glows warm amber. The whole ship is canted at a slight roll. Behind it: a swirling violet nebula. To the ship's left: the rose-pink eye from Shot 11, much larger now, watching from interstellar distance. Wide establishing shot, 8K hyper-realistic, deep starfield, anamorphic flare on the binary star.

**END FRAME:**
Camera has held the wide shot for ten seconds. The amber constellation of awakened pods has spread — now there are dozens of warm spots across the bow, a cluster of newly-awake pods. Inside one, our pod, the protagonist still stands with Elara. The rose-pink eye in deep space has slid closer by an arc-minute. A title fades up over the lower third of the frame: **"DISCHORDIAN SAGA"** in serif, set kerned to widescreen. Beneath it, smaller: *"Year One. Cycle One. Witness One."* Final composition.

**MOTION (2:45→3:00):**
