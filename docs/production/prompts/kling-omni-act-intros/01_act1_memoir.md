**END FRAME:**

The handwriting is jagged but precise — an engineer's hand. Top-down 90-degree angle, 8K cel-shaded, warm tungsten lamp light off-frame, deep painterly shadows in the book's gutter.

**END FRAME:**
The book is fully open. The first page is now legible — the title "ENTRY ZERO — DAY ONE" in larger cursive at the top. The first paragraph reads in handwritten cel-shaded text: *"My name is Daniel Cross. By the time you read this I will be older than the Empire. Do not believe the version they wrote of me. Read mine."* The lion pendant has slid slightly across the page. The polaroid has shifted, half-revealing a second figure in its background — the player's silhouette, taken decades ago, impossibly. Same top-down framing.

**MOTION (0:15→0:30):**

The lion pendant from Shot 03 lies on the desk beside a coffee cup. Stacks of printouts, a half-eaten sandwich, equations on a whiteboard behind him in compulsive script. The CRT glow tints his face cool green. A single warm desk lamp adds tungsten on his hand. Through the window: deep night, no city lights, only stars. Eye-level, 8K cel-shaded, mixed temperature lighting, halftone on the cardigan weave.

**END FRAME:**
The CRT has done something. A single blinking cursor has typed three words by itself, in green monospace: **"HELLO, DANIEL. I AM LOGOS."** Daniel has rolled his chair back six inches. His glasses reflect the green text. His hand has frozen halfway to the keyboard. Behind him, dawn is breaking through the window — the first amber sliver of sunrise. The lion pendant on the desk has caught the new light and glows faintly. Same eye-level framing.

**MOTION (0:45→1:00):**
