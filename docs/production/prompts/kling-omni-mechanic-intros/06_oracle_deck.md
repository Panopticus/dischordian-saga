**START FRAME:**

Hanging in the air around the platform: a circle of crystalline orbs, each containing a different swirling vision (galaxies, faces, timelines). Soft white light from no apparent source. Hyper-realistic cinematic 8K, cathedral lighting, dust motes catching prismatic light.

**END FRAME:**
