## Character reference — Malkia Ukweli

Canonical reference: `CANON_REV_7_ORACLE_VEX_EXPANSION.md` §5.6.9
(music direction) + §8.3 (Malkia Ukweli identity sequence).

**Visual identifiers (consistent across all 5 slides):**
- Mature female, deep-warm skin tone, hair pulled back in a
  practical low knot
- **Dark traveling coat with a purple scarf** — the scarf catches
  the cyan light from the hologram in every slide where the
  hologram is active (canon §8.3)
- Face: serious, watching. Not sad, not angry. The face of
  someone who is listening carefully to something very
  important and has not yet decided what to do with it. The
  only time her expression softens is slide 5, as she begins
  to sing.
- Age: read as mid-40s in the studio. Her Act 1 / Prelude
  Archives-scene appearance is 17,000 years later and will
  look different; these are her original-era studio shots.

**DO NOT render:**
- A cyberpunk or sci-fi-future studio aesthetic. This room is a
  musician's room — acoustic panels, analog console, a single
  pair of quality headphones on the desk. The technology that
  matters is the holographic playback unit; everything else is
  a working recording space that would look almost identical
  in a real 2020s studio.
- Any text on the hologram (Log 5 is audio + a ghost image of
  the Prince; never legible subtitles).
- Any other person. Malkia is alone in every slide.
- Bright cosmic / nebula lighting. The only cyan in the room
  is the hologram's edge glow and its reflection on her scarf.

## Slide 1 — Before she presses play

**Nano Banana 2 prompt:**

> Hyper-realistic cinematic still, 16:9, 4K. Interior of a small
> recording studio at late-night hour. A dark acoustic-panel wall
> is the background. Mid-ground: an analog recording console with
> brushed-metal surface, two large VU meters (needles at rest),
> four faders at the right edge, a single pair of black over-ear
> headphones draped over the console's right corner. Above the
> console, a waist-high **holographic playback unit** — a
> cylindrical pedestal about 40cm diameter with a dark surface;
> above it a dormant cyan rim, no hologram yet. Left foreground:
> **Malkia Ukweli**, mid-40s, deep-warm skin, hair in a low knot,
> wearing a dark travel coat over a black top, a **purple scarf**
> draped at her neck; she is seated at the console, one hand
> resting on the play control, looking at the dormant hologram
> pedestal. Expression: serious, watchful, not yet decided. Warm
> tungsten accent light from a single overhead practical lamp at
> the ceiling, plus the dim cyan rim on the pedestal. Deep space
> black `#010020` base outside the lit volume. Volumetric fog at
> ankle height, very subtle. Film grain. No rendered text. Shallow
> depth of field on Malkia's hand and face; console softened.

## Slide 2 — "I press play, and there you are"

**Nano Banana 2 prompt:**

> Same studio, two seconds later. The hologram is now **active**:
> a translucent cyan-edged figure of the Prince stands inside the
> pedestal's volume, sepia-toned (echoing the Prelude's flashback
> aesthetic), hands visible and open, face partially turned away,
> scan lines at 3% opacity. The Prince's hologram is roughly 30cm
> tall — a memory in a cupped hand, not life-size. Malkia's face
> is now tilted slightly up toward the hologram. Her expression
> has shifted: still quiet, but the weight of what she is hearing
> is beginning to land. Her hand has moved from the play control
> to the console's edge. The cyan rim of the pedestal is brighter
> now, and its reflection catches her **purple scarf**, tinting
> its edge cyan. Warm tungsten lamp still on above. Same fog,
> same film grain, same base. 16:9, 4K, no text.

## Slide 3 — Listening (tight)

**Nano Banana 2 prompt:**

> Same studio, 40 seconds later. Tight three-quarter close-up on
> Malkia's face, left of frame. The hologram's cyan rim-glow is
> visible as a soft out-of-focus column in the right background,
> sepia Prince figure unreadable at this focus distance. Malkia's
> eyes are reading the playback — focused on a specific moment in
> what she is hearing. No tears. Her lips are neutral. A single
> strand of hair has come loose from the knot. The cyan glow
> colors her cheekbone on the side nearest the hologram; the
> warm tungsten lamp above paints the other side. Shallow depth
> of field. 16:9, 4K, no rendered text. The shot's job is to
> tell the viewer that a specific line in the recording just
> landed, and she heard it.

## Slide 4 — "Flickering light in the dark of the stars"

**Nano Banana 2 prompt:**

> Same studio, another minute in. Medium three-quarter shot of
> Malkia seated at the console, now holding a **vocal microphone**
> at a professional condenser-mic stand that has been pulled close
> to her; her hand rests at the base of the stand but she has not
> yet sung into it. The hologram is still active in the background;
> the Prince's figure has shifted position slightly (canonical: his
> Log 5 hologram moves between movements). Malkia's expression is
> now the most still she has been — the weight of what she has
> decided to do has arrived. Her purple scarf catches the cyan
> rim-glow along one edge. Warm tungsten lamp above. Fog, grain,
> base as prior slides. 16:9, 4K, no text.

## Slide 5 — The first breath of the song

**Nano Banana 2 prompt:**

> Same studio, moments later. Close-up on Malkia's face with the
> vocal microphone center-frame, her lips parted mid-first-breath
> of the song, eyes closed. The Prince's hologram has gone dark —
> Log 5 ended with the canonical hard cut and there is no longer
> an image in the pedestal, only a faint cyan afterglow. Malkia
> is alone with her voice, the microphone, and the empty pedestal
> beyond it. Warm tungsten lamp brightest here — she has moved
> fully into her own register. The purple scarf drifts slightly
> with her breath. This is the first note of *Last Words*. Shallow
> depth of field on her face and the microphone; pedestal softened.
> Fog, grain, base. 16:9, 4K, no text.
