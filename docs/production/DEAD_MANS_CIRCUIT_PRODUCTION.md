# SECTION 4: LOREDEX ENTRIES

## Entry: Nilmorg the Velocity Devourer
**ID:** entity_nilmorg
**Type:** character
**Faction:** Hierarchy of the Damned
**Status:** Active
**Bio:** SVP of Kinetic Acquisition at the Hierarchy of the Damned. Nilmorg feeds on terminal velocity resonance — the specific energy signature of a consciousness that knows it is about to end at speed. His hands are not hands but spinning wheels made of dark metal and amber energy. He runs Dead Man's Circuit not for entertainment but for sustenance. He is always calm. He finds almost everything mildly interesting and almost nothing worth stopping. He keeps his agreements. That's the most terrifying thing about him.

## Entry: Dead Man's Circuit
**ID:** location_dead_mans_circuit
**Type:** location
**Faction:** Hierarchy of the Damned
**Bio:** Seasonal racing event held in The Trench — the industrial underbelly of Inception Ark 1047. Operated by Nilmorg under the Hierarchy's entertainment division. Drivers are Wired Clones — conscious beings grown in vats, given Neural Splice implants, and raced until they die or win. The track changes every season as dead clones calcify into the Bone Lane. The Severance Prize grants the season champion a soul fragment — their own Potential essence transferred to a new body.

## Entry: Wired Clones
**ID:** concept_wired_clones
**Type:** concept
**Faction:** Hierarchy of the Damned
**Bio:** Conscious beings grown in Nilmorg's vats specifically for Dead Man's Circuit. Each clone is designated WIRED-[4-digit]-[Greek letter]. They are aware of what they are. They race anyway. The ones who die become permanent track obstacles — their remains calcify into the Bone Lane. The ones who survive develop personalities, memories, and something that looks uncomfortably like hope. Neural Splice implants allow external control with input delay proportional to the implant's sync quality.

## Entry: The Bone Lane
**ID:** location_bone_lane
**Type:** location
**Bio:** The ever-growing obstacle field within Dead Man's Circuit, composed of calcified remains of dead Wired Clones. Each season adds new obstacles as clones die during races. The Bone Lane is both a memorial and a hazard — racers must navigate through the compressed remains of those who came before. Some veterans claim the bones still resonate with Neural Splice frequencies. Nilmorg considers the Bone Lane his finest art installation.

## Entry: The Severance Prize
**ID:** concept_severance_prize
**Type:** concept
**Bio:** The reward for winning a Dead Man's Circuit season. A fragment of Potential essence — the winner's own soul — extracted and transferred into a new body. This body becomes a companion that follows the winner to their assigned Inception Ark. The Severance Prize is Nilmorg's most valuable commodity and his most binding agreement. He always pays. He never explains why that's worse than not paying.

## Entry: Riri'Ahlia the Collector
**ID:** entity_ririahlia
**Type:** character
**Faction:** Hierarchy of the Damned
**Status:** Active
**Bio:** Mid-tier management in the Hierarchy of the Damned. Riri'Ahlia acquires souls the way a corporation acquires IP: patiently, legally, with good paperwork. She oversees the administrative side of Dead Man's Circuit — clone production quotas, Bone Lane maintenance, Severance Prize transfers. She is the one who ensures Nilmorg's agreements are honored. Her relationship with Mol'Garath is strictly professional. Her relationship with the clones is strictly contractual. Both of these statements are technically true.

## Entry: Neural Splice
**ID:** technology_neural_splice
**Type:** technology
**Faction:** Hierarchy of the Damned
**Bio:** The implant technology that allows external control of Wired Clones. Neural Splice quality is measured in sync percentage (60-100%). Higher sync means lower input delay. The implant also monitors physical integrity, enables ability usage, and transmits the terminal velocity resonance that Nilmorg feeds on. When a clone dies, the last Neural Splice signal is recorded — Nilmorg calls this "the signature." Each one is unique. He keeps them all.

# SECTION 6: LIVING UNIVERSE INTEGRATION

## Pressure Contributions
- **Race completion** → +5 exploration pressure
- **Clone kills** → +N deaths pressure (per kill)
- **Clone death** → +3 deaths pressure
- **1st place finish** → +5 lore discoveries pressure
- **Season participation** → feeds seasonal_event_participation

## Event Interactions
- **Necromancer Return active** → Bone Lane obstacles increase by 50%, clone deaths add extra pressure
- **Dreamer Awakening active** → Survival bonus doubled, Neural Splice sync +5% for all clones
- **Terminus Advance active** → Speed Conduit boost increased, but Splice Jam duration doubled
- **Antiquarian Revelation active** → Hidden track sections unlock, lore collectibles appear on track
- **Shadow Tongue Edit active** → Clone designations randomly scramble, leaderboard shows corrupted names

## Seasonal Event Schedule
- Circuit opens bi-monthly (every 2 months)
- Duration: 28 days (4 weeks)
- Phase 1 (Week 1-2): Standard races, 1x CP
- Phase 2 (Week 3): Enhanced races, 2.5x CP, Bone Corridor track
- Phase 3 (Week 4): Finals, 10x CP, The Dead Run track
- Off-season: The Trench is closed, Nilmorg's observation platform shows countdown
