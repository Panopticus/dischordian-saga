He manifests as a casino owner — charismatic, unpredictable, and deeply dangerous beneath the charm. He chose to run a casino because "entropy is the only honest game." His true identity is trust-gated: at first he appears as a bartender/host, then a casino boss, then finally a cosmic entity. His visual design must support all three readings of the same character.*

**Character Design Notes:**
- **Build:** Lean, angular, predatory grace. Moves like a card shark. Never still.
- **Face:** Sharp features, high cheekbones, a grin that is always slightly too wide. Eyes that see probability, not light.
- **Attire (Bartender Phase):** Dark vest over rolled-sleeve shirt, amber cufflinks, gold pocket watch chain. Working-class cosmic.
- **Attire (Casino Boss Phase):** Full dark suit with golden pinstripe, void-energy lapel pin (skull with dice eyes), obsidian cufflinks.
- **Attire (Ne-Yon Reveal Phase):** The suit becomes living entropy — fabric dissolving into void energy at the edges, golden geometric patterns flowing across the surface like probability equations being solved in real-time, eyes become solid gold.
- **Signature Color:** Amber-gold (#FFB800) with entropy purple (#8B00FF) accents
- **Voice Aesthetic:** If his voice were visible, it would look like golden smoke curling through the air.

## SHADOW TONGUE CORRUPTION (1% Random UI Label Corruption)

*Randomly, 1% of the time, a UI label changes to something slightly wrong. "Dream Tokens" becomes "Dream Takens." "Armory" becomes "Memory." "Elara" becomes "Senator." These are Shadow Tongue editing reality. If the player clicks the corrupted text, they earn Antiquarian trust.*

### ST-001: Text Corruption Effect (CSS/Shader Reference)
**Purpose:** Visual specification for how corrupted text should appear

**Normal State:** Standard UI text in the game's amber/cyan palette.

**Corrupted State Specification:**
- Text color shifts from standard to indigo (#4B0082) with a 0.5-second transition
- A faint indigo glow (2px blur, 30% opacity) appears behind the corrupted word
- The corrupted text has a micro-animation: letters shift 1px left-right at random intervals (not uniform shimmer — organic wrongness)
- A barely visible watermark effect behind the corrupted word — Shadow Tongue's face formed from flowing text, 5% opacity, only visible if you're looking for it
- When hovered, the corruption intensifies: indigo glow brightens to 60%, letters shift faster, a faint whisper sound cue triggers
- When clicked: a brief indigo flash (100ms), the text reverts to correct, and a small "+1 Antiquarian Trust" notification appears in green

## MORALITY-DRIVEN UI MORPHING

*As the player's morality shifts, the entire UI theme subtly transforms. This is not a toggle — it's a gradient. The player FEELS their alignment through the interface itself.*

### MR-001: Humanity Path UI (Morality +60 to +100)
**Purpose:** Visual specification for the warm, organic, Elara-aligned interface state

**Color Shifts:**
- Primary accent: shifts from neutral amber (#FFB800) toward warm cyan (#22d3ee)
- Background: warms from void black (#050510) toward deep navy (#0a1628)
- Text: gains subtle warmth — pure white (#ffffff) shifts to warm white (#fff8f0)
- Success states: brighter, more saturated greens

**Material Shifts:**
- UI panel borders: gain organic curvature — border-radius increases by 20%
- Glass surfaces: warmer tint — amber/cyan blend in glass blur
- Holographic elements: more stable, less static, cleaner projections
- Elara's hologram: brighter, more solid, fewer scanline artifacts — she is more real when you choose humanity

**Ambient Effects:**
- Particle color: warm golden motes with occasional cyan sparks
- Ambient light: warm amber fill light from below (sunrise simulation)
- Sound aesthetic: warmer reverb, organic resonance

### MR-002: Machine Path UI (Morality -60 to -100)
**Purpose:** Visual specification for the cold, angular, Human-aligned interface state

**Color Shifts:**
- Primary accent: shifts from neutral amber (#FFB800) toward cold red (#ff1744)
- Background: cools from void black (#050510) toward digital black (#000000) — true black, no warmth
- Text: sharpens — pure white becomes cold blue-white (#e0e8ff)
- Error states: more frequent, harsher — red flashes brighter

**Material Shifts:**
- UI panel borders: angular, sharper — border-radius decreases by 30%, edges become precise
- Glass surfaces: cold blue tint, more reflective, less transparent
- Holographic elements: more static, glitch artifacts, red interference patterns
- The Human's voice: clearer, less distorted — he is more real when you choose machine

**Ambient Effects:**
- Particle color: cold blue motes with red electrical sparks
- Ambient light: cooler, directional — harsh overhead lighting (interrogation)
- Sound aesthetic: sharper, more metallic, digital reverb
