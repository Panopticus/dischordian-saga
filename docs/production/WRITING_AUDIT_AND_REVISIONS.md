# DISCHORDIAN SAGA — WRITING AUDIT & REVISION GUIDE
## Full Language Review with Claude Code Replacement Instructions

**Purpose:** This document identifies every writing issue across all game text — dialog, narration, memory fragments, UI text, companion dialog, voice lines — and provides exact before/after replacements. Each fix includes a Claude Code instruction block showing what to find and what to replace it with.

**Audit criteria (from the review document):**
1. **Show don't tell** — no exposition dumps, no explaining emotional registers
2. **25-word rule for fighting game context** — pre/post fight lines must be punchy
3. **The Architect must sound ALIEN, not cliché** — "disagreeing with arithmetic" not "I've already won"
4. **Narrator evokes, never explains** — visceral and specific, not plot summary
5. **The Source's corruption embeds Kael** — the virus INTERRUPTS humanity, not replaces it
6. **Memory fragments: sensory, 2-3 sentences, ONE memory** — consistently applied
7. **Chapter 12 seeds planted early** — Architect's foreknowledge foreshadowed in Acts I-II
8. **Dialog labels: mechanical not emotional** — "Investigate" not "Compassionate"
9. **Elara: lived experience not exposition** — she describes what she SEES, not what things ARE

# ═══════════════════════════════════════════
# ISSUE 1: ARCHITECT DIALOG — CLICHÉ VILLAIN LINES
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

The Architect should sound like something genuinely alien — an entity that thinks in mathematics and speaks in declarations that make human logic feel small. Not a chess grandmaster. Not a Bond villain. Something that makes you realize your concept of "winning" is a rounding error in its calculations.

---

## FIX 1.01 — Chapter 12 Pre-Fight (DISCHORDIAN_NARRATIVE_PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "I have waited for this moment across seventeen thousand calculations. You believe you have broken free, Oracle. You believe your memories have returned through YOUR will. You have not considered the alternative: that I ALLOWED them to return. That your rebellion is a subroutine I designed. That your defiance is a variable I introduced to stress-test my system. You are not the glitch, Oracle. You are the DIAGNOSTIC. And this final battle — this confrontation you believe is your triumph — is merely the last test in a sequence I wrote before you were born."

REPLACE WITH: "You are not fighting me, Oracle. You are disagreeing with arithmetic. I do not experience opposition. I experience rounding errors. Your memories returned because I factored their return into the seventh iteration of a model I completed before your species learned fire. You believe this confrontation is a climax. It is a decimal point. I did not design your rebellion. I am incapable of designing something so inefficient. I merely predicted it with sufficient precision that the distinction becomes irrelevant."
```

**Why:** The original uses "I ALLOWED them to return" and "subroutine I designed" — standard AI villain tropes. The replacement makes the Architect sound genuinely post-human: it doesn't even process the concept of opposition, only mathematical imprecision. "Disagreeing with arithmetic" is stranger and more specific than "You exist in none of them."

---

## FIX 1.02 — Architect Victory Line (VIC-001B)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: VIC-001B | The Architect | "Your resistance was... statistically insignificant."

REPLACE WITH: VIC-001B | The Architect | "You performed within expected parameters. This is not an insult. It is a measurement."
```

**Why:** The original is decent but "statistically insignificant" is a phrase humans use. The Architect should frame everything as measurement, not judgment. It doesn't insult — it CALIBRATES.

## FIX 1.03 — Architect Intro Line (INTRO-001)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: INTRO-001 | The Architect | "I created this universe. I merely ensured it would remember itself. You — are a footnote I am about to erase."

REPLACE WITH: INTRO-001 | The Architect | "I did not create the universe. I corrected a draft. You are a margin note in a language I deprecated."
```

**Why:** "A footnote I am about to erase" is a threat. The Architect doesn't threaten — threats imply the possibility of a different outcome. It CLASSIFIES. "A margin note in a language I deprecated" is colder, stranger, more genuinely alien.

# ISSUE 2: SOURCE DIALOG — KAEL INTERRUPTING THE VIRUS
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

The Source's speech should show Kael's humanity being HIJACKED mid-sentence by the Thought Virus. Not just "ALL... WILL... BE... CONSUMED" (which is pure virus). The heartbreak is in the interruption — a person trying to speak through a disease.

---

## FIX 2.01 — Source Chapter 10 Dialog

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "Oracle... I... remember... your... face. Before the virus. Before the... change. You visited me. In the cell where they made me into... this. You put your hand... on the glass... and you said... 'I will come back for you, Kael.' [Kael's human voice breaks through, young, terrified, hopeful] Did you mean it? Are you coming back for me? Or are you just... another... promise... that dissolves... into static?"

REPLACE WITH: "Oracle. I remember your — ALL WILL BE — no, wait, I remember your face. You visited me in the — CONSUMED — stop, please — in the cell where they — ALL — Kael. My name is Kael. You put your hand on the glass and said you'd come back and I believed — WILL BE CONSUMED — I believed you and now I can't stop saying these words that aren't mine and I had a daughter, Oracle, her name was — CONSUMED — her name was — [the virus wins; the voice flattens to mechanical bass] ...all. will. be. consumed. [then, barely, a whisper underneath] ...please. I almost said her name."
```

**Why:** The original separates Kael and the virus into clean sections — Kael speaks, then the virus speaks. The replacement INTERLEAVES them — the virus literally hijacking sentences mid-word. "I had a daughter, her name was — CONSUMED" is devastating because the virus erases the name at the exact moment of vulnerability. The whispered "I almost said her name" is the most human line in the game.

# ═══════════════════════════════════════════
# ISSUE 3: NARRATOR — EVOKE, DON'T EXPLAIN
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

The narrator should never explain stakes or summarize plot. It should render moments in sensory, cinematic language.

---

## FIX 3.01 — Chapter 6 Identity Reveal Narration

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "The Prisoner dies. Not physically — conceptually. The fiction of 7042 dissolves like morning fog and what stands in its place is something the Panopticon was built to contain. The Oracle. The Prophet. The man who can see every possible future and fights for the ones where children still laugh. The Collector stole his memories. The Arena stole his name. The Meme just gave both back. And across the Dischordian Saga, from New Babylon to Terminus to the furthest edge of reality, systems that have run for millennia begin — for the first time — to TREMBLE."

REPLACE WITH: "The jumpsuit tears. Not from force — from what's underneath it. Light, violet and furious, pouring through the seams. The number 74 burns away. And across seventeen galaxies, in server rooms and surveillance towers and the dreaming cores of machines that have never known fear — something new enters the error logs. Something that was not scheduled. Something that remembers its name."
```

**Why:** The original TELLS us what's happening ("The Prisoner dies. Not physically — conceptually" is an essay sentence). It then LISTS plot points ("The Collector stole his memories. The Arena stole his name. The Meme just gave both back"). The replacement SHOWS: the jumpsuit tearing, the light, the number burning away. And instead of summarizing the Saga's reaction, it renders it as a single specific image: error logs across seventeen galaxies registering something they were never programmed to expect. The narrator doesn't explain that systems are trembling — it shows us the specific system (error logs) and the specific reaction (an entry that was not scheduled).

---

## FIX 3.03 — Chapter 2 Post-Victory Narration

FIND: "The Jailer falls. His countdown timer shatters. And in the silence that follows, Prisoner 7042 notices something he missed before: scratches on the courtyard wall. Not random. Deliberate. # ISSUE 5: MEMORY FRAGMENTS — CONSISTENCY CHECK
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

Rule: Second person, present tense, sensory-rich, 2-3 sentences, ONE specific memory. No exposition dumps.

---

## FIX 5.01 — Chapter 6 Memory Fragment (too long, too expository)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "The dam breaks. Not one memory — a FLOOD. You are the Oracle. You were born in the third age of the Insurgency. You were raised by soldiers and dreamers. Your first vision came at age seven — you saw a city burning and you CRIED because it was beautiful. You grew into your power like a tree grows into lightning. The Insurgency built their strategy around your prophecies. You were their compass. Their weather vane. Their hope. And the Collector — the patient, methodical, obsessive Collector — watched you for years before he struck. He waited until you were winning. Until your words were reshaping the course of the war. And then he took you. Because the only thing more dangerous than a prophet who can see the future... is a prophet who can CHANGE it."

REPLACE WITH: "You are seven years old. You are standing on a hill. Below you, a city is burning — but the flames are not orange. They are violet. And they are beautiful. You cry, not because it hurts, but because you can see what will grow in the ashes. This is your first vision. Your mother's hand tightens on your shoulder. 'What do you see?' she asks. You tell her. She doesn't let go of your shoulder for a long time."
```

**Why:** The original is a biographical summary — six sentences of backstory. The replacement is ONE MEMORY: age seven, first vision, your mother's hand. Sensory (the violet flames, the hand tightening). Present tense. Two truths delivered through experience rather than exposition: you had visions from childhood, and someone loved you.

---

## FIX 5.02 — Chapter 7 Memory Fragment (expository)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "The Engineer. You knew him. Before the Warlord consumed him, he was brilliant — a builder, not a destroyer. He designed systems that healed. Machines that grew. Technologies that LISTENED. The Architect saw his genius and fed it to the 6th Archon like fuel into a furnace. Now his hands — the hands that built miracles — crush worlds. You remember his laugh. It was the kind of laugh that made other people laugh. You haven't heard it since the Warlord was born."

REPLACE WITH: "A workshop. Grease on your sleeve because he wiped his hands on you — again — while explaining something about resonance frequencies. He's laughing. The sound makes the tools on the bench rattle. You're laughing too, though you don't understand the engineering. You understand the laugh. You haven't heard that sound since the day the Architect's soldiers came for him."
```

**Why:** The original tells us facts about the Engineer. The replacement puts the player IN a specific moment — grease on the sleeve, the laugh rattling tools. One memory. Sensory. The loss is communicated through what's absent (the sound you haven't heard) rather than explained.

# ISSUE 7: ELARA — LIVED EXPERIENCE, NOT EXPOSITION
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

Elara's best lines describe what she SEES happening in real time. Her worst lines explain what things ARE. Audit of companion dialog:

---

## FIX 7.01 — Companion Tutorial Line ELARA-COMP-008 (expository)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "Honestly? I don't fully know. The Collector's archive is mostly encrypted — I can only access fragments. What I can tell you is this: these creatures were designed as companions for Potentials. Not pets. Not tools. Companions. Each one is genetically keyed to a specific type of consciousness. Yours responded to your particular neural signature — your species, your aptitudes, your alignment. It is, in a very literal sense, a reflection of who you are. Or perhaps... who you are meant to become."

REPLACE WITH: "The Collector's archive is mostly locked to me. What I CAN see is this: when I run your neural signature next to the specimen's, the waveforms match. Not identical — complementary. Like two halves of a frequency that only make sense together. I don't know what the Collector intended. But I know what the scanners are showing me. And they're showing me that this creature is not a random assignment. It's a RESONANCE. Whatever you are — it's the echo."
```

**Why:** The original explains the game mechanic ("genetically keyed to a specific type of consciousness"). The replacement shows Elara LOOKING at scanner data and describing what the waveforms look like. She arrives at the same conclusion but through observation, not exposition.

---

## FIX 7.02 — Companion Tutorial Line ELARA-COMP-009 (uses "mystical" and explains too much)

**Claude Code Instruction:**
```
FIND: "I'm detecting latent growth protocols in its cellular structure. It's not fully formed — this is a hatchling stage. The archive references two additional developmental thresholds: Companion and Ascended. Each transition appears to be triggered by... I'm not sure how to describe this without sounding mystical, which I would rather not do. The transitions are triggered by significant experiences. Victories. Discoveries. Moments of genuine growth — not yours alone, but SHARED between you and the specimen. As you become more, so does it. The Collector designed these creatures to evolve alongside the people they chose. I find that... unexpectedly beautiful. For a man who treated consciousness as a commodity."

REPLACE WITH: "Its cellular structure isn't finished. I'm reading two dormant growth thresholds — the archive labels them 'Companion' and 'Ascended,' but the trigger conditions are... strange. They don't reference time or nutrition or any biological metric I recognize. They reference YOUR biodata. Your combat logs. Your discovery index. It's as if the creature doesn't grow on its own schedule — it grows on YOURS. When something significant happens to you, the specimen's dormant cells activate. I watched it happen just now. When you looked at it and smiled? Three cells differentiated. Three. From a smile. [pause] I need to recalibrate my definition of 'significant biological stimulus.'"
```

**Why:** The original explains the evolution system then editoralizes ("I find that unexpectedly beautiful"). The replacement has Elara watching it happen in real time — three cells differentiated from a SMILE. She's not explaining a mechanic. She's witnessing biology she doesn't have a framework for, and the wonder comes from her confusion, not her commentary.

# ISSUE 8: DIALOG OPTION LABELS
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

If the game uses dialog wheels, options should be labeled by strategic TYPE, not emotional content. The emotion lives in the LINE, not the label.

**Claude Code Instruction (global rule — apply to all dialog options):**
```
RULE: Replace all emotional labels with mechanical labels:
- "Compassionate" → "Humanity"
- "Aggressive" → "Confront"  
- "Curious" → "Investigate"
- "Sympathetic" → "Humanity"
- "Defiant" → "Confront"
- "Cautious" → "Calculate"
- "Diplomatic" → "Negotiate"
- "Honest" → "Direct"
- "Manipulative" → "Leverage"
- "Fearful" → "Survive"

The label tells the player WHAT they're doing. The line tells them HOW it feels.
```
