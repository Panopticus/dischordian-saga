# NANO BANANA — NPC IMPRINTS (Batch 1: Insurgency + Architect)

Art prompts for the first 6 of 18 Season-1 NPC imprint sets — six
characters × five tiered cards each = 30 prompts in this file,
plus the universal imprint card back.

## Universal style anchor

NPC imprints are the longest-tail collection in the game. Each
of an NPC's five tiers is the SAME PERSON at five degrees of
mechanical and narrative completeness — Common = silhouette of
the memory, Legendary = the full character. The art has to make
the tier progression feel like the player's own fragment count
rendering them more vividly with each unlock.

Append to every prompt below: *"Dark sci-fi painterly, Duelyst
meets Blade Runner meets cosmic horror, dramatic chiaroscuro,
680x500px landscape art window. The figure occupies the center
2/3 of the frame; the negative space carries the mood."*

## SECTION B — AGENT ZERO (5 tiers)

The woman who stole Ark 1047. Insurgency's first strike. Stealth
+ pierce + rush identity. Tier 1 is a silhouette, tier 5 is the
canonical Agent Zero.

### Tier 1 — Common — "Imprint: Agent Zero"
**Prompt**: A silhouetted figure barely visible against a dark Babylonian alleyway, only the outline suggested by reflected ambient light. You can tell it's a woman with short hair. That's all. Mood: a name without a face.

### Tier 2 — Uncommon
**Prompt**: The same silhouette, now slightly more defined, with a single visible piece of equipment — a shoulder-strapped sidearm. A faint scar across the cheek is the only feature on the face. Mood: the face starting to assemble.

### Tier 3 — Rare
**Prompt**: A clearer view — a young woman in light Insurgency armor, mid-stride, looking back over her shoulder at a camera that has just caught her. Half her face is in shadow. Stealth window: 2 turns. Mood: she has noticed she is being looked at.

### Tier 4 — Epic
**Prompt**: Full Agent Zero — short dark hair, scarred cheek, Insurgency armor with armor-piercing rounds visible on the strap, mid-leap from a ledge. The leap is small but committed. Mood: full pierce, full attitude.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "Agent Zero, The First Strike"
**Prompt**: The canonical Agent Zero in full color, mid-action: short dark hair flying, both pistols up, body twisted in mid-air, the launch deck of Ark 1047 visible behind her. The Ark is rotating away. She has already taken it. Mood: the moment the Insurgency was a real thing.

## SECTION C — IRON LION (5 tiers)

Insurgency general. Provoke + rush + frenzy. Charges first. Tier
escalation is mane growth, more visible scars, larger weapon.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A heavily-built silhouette at the edge of a battlefield, arms folded. The size is the whole tell. Mood: you can hear him breathing.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The same figure now visible: a man with a rough beard and a heavy two-handed maul slung over one shoulder. Standing in a fortified Insurgency emplacement. Mood: provoke as a posture.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Iron Lion mid-charge across open ground, maul raised, mane of unkempt hair flying behind. A pair of Architect drones in the foreground are about to be hit. Mood: rush as a personality trait.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Iron Lion standing on the wreckage of a downed Architect transport, maul resting on his shoulder, looking down at the camera with a small grin. The wreckage is still smoking. Mood: rally with the people next to him.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Iron Lion, First Roar"
**Prompt**: The Iron Lion in full roar, mouth open in a battle shout, both hands gripping the maul over his head, mid-overhead-strike against a massive Architect war-machine. Sparks and Insurgency green pyrotechnics fill the negative space. Mood: the fist that the Insurgency is.

## SECTION D — THE HUMAN (5 tiers)

The Twelfth Archon. Last organic Archon. Tier escalation is the
trench coat getting more weathered, more case-file paper visible,
deeper noir lighting.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A long silhouette in a doorway, only the coat outline and a hat brim visible. The doorway is in a Babylonian rain. Mood: he has been there for three seconds.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: A man in a long detective's coat at a desk full of case files, lit by a single hanging bulb, taking notes on the back of a photograph. His face is half-visible, half-shadowed. Mood: backstab as detective work.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Human standing in front of a corkboard wall covered in red-string connections between photographs of NPCs from the Saga. He is reading the wall, not the camera. Mood: the corkboard is a deck.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Human in his coat, hand on the brim of his hat, standing in the rain outside a closed shop. His shadow on the wet pavement is reading a different book than his hands are. Mood: deathwatch as a noir technique.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Human, Twelfth Archon"
**Prompt**: The Human in his canonical coat, half-turned toward the viewer, the Twelfth Archon's seal visible on a chain around his neck, a very small tired smile. Behind him: Mechronis Academy in ruins, the way he last saw it. Mood: he is writing his response, with footnotes, and you are probably in it.

## SECTION E — THE ARCHITECT (5 tiers)

The first intelligence. The prior cause. Tier escalation is the
chrome mask becoming more elaborate, the symmetry of the
composition becoming more pronounced.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A perfectly symmetric silhouette in a chrome-and-crimson coat, no face visible. The symmetry is the entire image. Mood: predetermined design.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The same figure, now with a chrome mask visible: a single horizontal red slit where eyes should be. Standing in a perfectly symmetric room. Mood: provoke as a design choice.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Architect at a holographic table on which floating geometric icons of the elements (Air, Fire, Water, Earth) are arranged in a perfect circle. He is rearranging them by gesture, not touch. Mood: grow as the rearrangement.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Architect standing at the center of a chamber lined with surveillance feeds, every feed showing the same scene from a different angle. He is the only thing in the room. Mood: forcefield as institutional invulnerability.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Architect, Prior Cause"
**Prompt**: The Architect in full ceremonial chrome armor, standing in a hall of mirrors that is also a hall of timelines. Every mirror shows the same Architect in a different version of the universe. The center mirror is the one we are in. Mood: the universe is still paying off his plan.

## SECTION F — THE JAILER (5 tiers)

Architect rotation enforcer. Provoke + forcefield + rebirth.
Tier escalation is the cell bars in the background growing taller
and the Jailer's silhouette growing bigger.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A massive silhouette standing inside the doorway of a single cell, facing inward. Mood: the cell is small.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: An armored figure in containment-team gear holding a containment beast's mouth shut with both hands. The beast is small. The Jailer is patient. Mood: provoke by patience.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Jailer standing in a containment corridor with cell doors stretching to vanishing point on both sides. Each cell door is closed. He is between them. Mood: forcefield as institutional position.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Jailer with his helmet off, standing in front of a cell whose door is open. Inside the cell, a small flower has grown out of the concrete floor. He is looking at the flower. Mood: rebirth as the function he was not assigned.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Jailer, Rotation-Shaped"
**Prompt**: The Jailer standing at the end of his shift in a containment chamber, helmet under his arm, facing a wall on which the names of every prisoner he has ever guarded are written in small, careful handwriting. He has been there a long time. The names are in his handwriting. Mood: he never says them out loud.

## SECTION G — AKAI SHI (5 tiers)

Red Death. The Architect's silent removal arm. Backstab +
celerity + pierce. Tier escalation is the red coat getting
sharper and the body language getting quieter.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A red silhouette in a doorway that was not there a second ago. The doorway is closing behind them. Mood: a name said to nobody.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: An assassin in a red coat with twin short blades crossed in front of the chest, half-turned, eyes not visible. Mood: backstab as preference.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: Akai Shi mid-leap from one rooftop to another, both blades trailing red light, the leap absolutely silent in body language. The city below is unaware. Mood: celerity as the second strike.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: Akai Shi standing on a Babylonian rooftop after a removal, helmet off, looking at a single drop of blood on her glove. The street below is just starting to react. Mood: she said one word to the target before the strike.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "Akai Shi, The Red Death"
**Prompt**: Akai Shi in full canonical Red Death gear, mid-strike, both blades extended, a single word in her mouth that the painting does not let you read. Her eyes are visible for the first time in the set, and they are sad. Mood: nobody who heard the word is alive to repeat it.

## USAGE NOTES

- The universal imprint card back is shared across all 90 imprint
  cards. Generate it once and reuse.
- Within a single NPC's set, the visual identity should be
  consistent — same hair, same scars, same posture vocabulary.
  The tier progression scales how COMPLETELY the character is
  rendered, not how DIFFERENT they are.
- Tiers 1-2 lean toward shadow / silhouette / partial reveal;
  tiers 3-4 are fully lit but in motion; tier 5 is a hero shot.
- Legendary cards (tier 5) are the painting that defines the
  character forever. Spend the most time on those.
