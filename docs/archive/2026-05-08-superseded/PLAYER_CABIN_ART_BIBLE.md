# Player Cabin — Art Production Bible

> **Style**: Hyper-realistic science fiction. Blade Runner 2049 meets Dead Space meets Mass Effect.
> Photorealistic lighting, volumetric fog, real material textures (brushed titanium, frosted glass, bioluminescent organics).
> **Tools**: NanoBanna 2 (generation), Magnific AI (upscaling), Remove.bg/Photopea (transparency)
> **Negative prompts for ALL assets**: `cartoon, anime, low quality, blurry, text artifacts, watermark, illustration, painting style, flat lighting, oversaturated`
