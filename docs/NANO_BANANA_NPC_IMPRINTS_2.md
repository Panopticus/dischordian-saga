# NANO BANANA — NPC IMPRINTS (Batch 2: Dreamer + New Babylon)

Six NPC imprint sets × five tiers each = 30 prompts. Style anchor
and universal card back are documented in `NANO_BANANA_NPC_IMPRINTS_1.md`.

---

## SECTION A — THE WHITE ORACLE (5 tiers)

The Architect's sundered twin. Suspended in a processing loop and
still broadcasting. Tier escalation is the holographic loop
becoming more visible and the Oracle becoming MORE present
through the loop, not less.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A veiled silhouette behind a wall of running code, only the head shape visible. The code is moving fast enough that the silhouette pulses. Mood: a name in a process.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Oracle's veil partially visible — a translucent fabric over a face that is looking directly at the viewer through it. Dispel symbol on the temple. Mood: she dispelled your buff.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The White Oracle suspended upside-down inside a holographic processing lattice. Her hair falls toward the bottom of the frame. One hand extends toward the viewer through a gap in the lattice. Mood: peeks at the top card of your deck.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Oracle in the lattice with both arms extended, the lattice now visibly straining around her. She is flying inside the cage. Mood: dispel + flying as the cage's failure.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The White Oracle, Still Broadcasting"
**Prompt**: The Oracle inside the lattice, arms wide, broadcasting visible signal lines outward in all directions through the lattice walls. The lattice is cracking. Behind her, a faint second figure — the Architect — is half-turned away, refusing to look. Mood: the loop has been leaking since the day it closed. This card is a leak.

## SECTION B — THE NECROMANCER (5 tiers)

Death as a kindness. Walks away across dissolving cards. Tier
escalation is the field of bone-white flowers behind him growing
denser.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A robed silhouette walking away from the viewer across a grey field. A single small bone-white flower behind him in his footprint. Mood: he has not turned around.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Necromancer in mid-stride, robe trailing. Two bone-white flowers behind him now. He is taller than expected. Mood: deathwatch as the field he is walking through.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Necromancer at the edge of the grey field, the dissolving cards visible underfoot, a small cluster of bone-white flowers behind him. He is checking the next step. Mood: rebirth as the second walk.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Necromancer turned three-quarters toward the viewer, hood up, face still hidden. The bone-white flowers are everywhere now. He is mid-strike with a thin curved blade — the strike is also a flower-planting motion. Mood: heals on damage dealt.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Necromancer, Mercy-Shaped"
**Prompt**: The Necromancer fully turned to face the viewer, hood still up, face still not visible — but you can feel that if you saw it you would recognize him. The field of bone-white flowers stretches to vanishing point in every direction. Drain has stopped feeling like draining. Mood: he unmakes his friends as a kindness now.

## SECTION C — THE DREAMER (5 tiers)

Prophetic vision. The half of the first intelligence that sees
the future. Tier escalation is the veil getting more translucent
and a starfield bleeding through the body.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A veiled silhouette hovering above the floor of a temple antechamber, feet not touching down. Mood: she has not yet decided to be here.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Dreamer with a star-chart scroll unfurling itself in front of her. Flying. The scroll is reading itself. Mood: dreamt the card an hour ago.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Dreamer in mid-air, robes flowing, both hands holding the corners of a probability map that is currently displaying an event that has not happened yet. Mood: dispel as foresight.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Dreamer with the starfield visible THROUGH the gaps in her robes, like the figure is partially a doorway and partially a body. Forcefield as starfield density. Mood: dodges by having dreamed the dodge.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Dreamer, Sundered Twin"
**Prompt**: The Dreamer fully revealed: a veiled female figure between two pillars labeled PAST and FUTURE, both arms raised, the entire scene rendered with one half in upright orientation and the other half in subtly reversed orientation. The Architect's silhouette is faintly visible in a third pillar in the deep background. Mood: the half that looks backward through time.

## SECTION D — ADJUDICATOR LOCKE (5 tiers)

The last Archon who still makes decisions as if decisions matter.
New Babylon faction. Provoke + heal + silence. Tier escalation is
the desk getting bigger and the case files getting more organized.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A man in a jurist's coat at a tidy desk, signing a single document with a heavy pen. The desk is the only thing in the room. Mood: tired and incorruptible.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The same man standing behind the desk, hand on a gavel. The gavel has not been used yet. The room has gone quiet. Mood: provoke as authority in the room.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: Locke in a Babylonian courthouse foyer, holding a folded writ in one hand, the other hand raised to a young clerk who is bringing him another document. The clerk is hesitant. Mood: heal general as protective authority.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: Locke in his ceremonial robes standing in the chamber doorway, mid-sentence, addressing a gallery that is not visible. The doorway is the only frame. Mood: forcefield as the institution he is.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "Locke, the Last Adjudicator"
**Prompt**: Adjudicator Locke seated on a throne that is also a judge's bench, gavel raised, mid-strike. Behind him, a wall of stacked law books that doubles as the Babylonian skyline. He is the only person in frame and he is making the decision. Mood: the gavel comes down once.

## SECTION E — FOUCAULT (5 tiers)

Surveillance theorist. Knows where the cameras are not. New
Babylon faction. Dispel + stealth + silence. Tier escalation is
the surveillance map in the background becoming more annotated.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A reader at a desk in a Babylonian archive, surrounded by stacks of bound surveillance reports. He is annotating one with a red pen. Mood: he is not in the surveillance logs because he wrote them.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: Foucault standing in front of a wall-sized map of the Babylonian sector, marking a small X in red ink at a specific point. Dispel as the X. Mood: he reads your buff and crosses it out.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: Foucault in a Babylonian alleyway at night, deliberately stepping into the blind spot of a nearby surveillance camera. The blind spot is rendered as a faint red triangle. Stealth as architecture. Mood: he installed the blind spot two years ago.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: Foucault in his coat reading a small notebook, walking down a street where every camera has subtly turned away. The cameras agree with him, in the abstract. Mood: stealth-2 as social engineering.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "Foucault, Schedule of Glances"
**Prompt**: Foucault standing at the center of a vast wall covered in a hand-drawn schedule labeled SCHEDULE OF GLANCES, with arrows and timings in red ink connecting hundreds of small annotations. He is reading his own work. The Babylonians are not. Mood: silence the enemy general by pulling the page they were going to read.

## SECTION F — THE COLLECTOR (5 tiers)

Attachment as bondage. Eleven centuries of trying to want nothing
and failing. New Babylon faction. Drain + forcefield + stun. Tier
escalation is the room becoming more crowded with display cases.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A man in an old velvet coat sitting in a cluttered antiquarian's study, surrounded by glass display cases. He is looking at one specific case, the one with a small dried flower in it. Mood: drain as small attachment.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Collector standing among his cases, holding a new acquisition — a single gold ring — and considering where to display it. Three rings already on his fingers. Mood: drain as the small amount of everything he handles.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Collector in his throne room, the throne now visibly built from stacked display cases. A bronze chain hangs from the chair to his ankle. He is offering the viewer a smaller chain with an apologetic smile. Stun as the offer. Mood: it is sincerely optional.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Collector standing in front of his collection, the collection extending behind him to vanishing point. Forcefield as the room's physical reluctance to let anything leave. Mood: drain + forcefield + stun.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Collector, Eleven Centuries"
**Prompt**: The Collector in his greatest moment: standing in the center of his collection, hands open, offering everything to the viewer at once. Behind him, every glass case has a small handwritten label naming the person each object came from. The labels are all in his handwriting. He has been writing them for eleven centuries. He remembers all of them. Mood: stun as the kind of grief that makes the room briefly stop.

## USAGE NOTES

- See `NANO_BANANA_NPC_IMPRINTS_1.md` for the universal style
  anchor and shared imprint card back.
- The Dreamer's tier-5 reveal must subtly mirror the Architect's
  tier-5 image (from batch 1) — they are the same intelligence
  sundered. The mirror should be visual, not literal.
- The Necromancer's face is never shown in any of his five tiers;
  the recognition is felt by the player, not seen.
- Locke's tier 5 is one of the few legendary cards in this set
  where the character is actively striking — most are static
  hero shots. This is on purpose.
