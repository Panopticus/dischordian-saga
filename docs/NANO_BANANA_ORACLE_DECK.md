# NANO BANANA — ORACLE DECK (23 cards)

Art prompts for the 23-card Dischordian Oracle Deck authored in
Phase E. Every prompt is tuned for the Nano Banana pipeline and
paired with the card's authored lore so art and text stay locked
together.

**Format**: 750x1050px (5:7 TCG standard). The Oracle Deck is NOT
a normal TCG set — its frame is vertical portrait not landscape,
because these are tarot cards first and game cards second.

**Style anchor**: Aleister Crowley's Thoth Tarot painted by Lady
Frieda Harris as the top-line aesthetic ancestor. Geometric
symbolism, saturated jewel tones, sacred geometry in the
background, figures rendered with visible brushstroke energy.
Dischordian cyberpunk lens dropped on top — holographic overlays,
signal-glitch edges, chromatic aberration on key symbols, deep
space behind the figure instead of a traditional tarot sky.

**Universal framing**: Each card is a vertical portrait of its
archetype figure (or symbolic scene) centered in the frame, with
sacred-geometry line work behind. Numeral (0-22) appears top
center in Art Deco Roman numerals. Card name in the bottom
cartouche in the same font as the TCG card frames.

**Prompt suffix** (append to every card prompt below):
*"Thoth Tarot by Lady Frieda Harris aesthetic, Dischordian
cyberpunk painterly style, vertical portrait, sacred geometry
background, holographic overlays, rich jewel tones, dramatic
chiaroscuro, 750x1050px."*

## CARD 0 — THE PRISONER (Fool)
**Palette**: Bone white (#e7e5e4), raw amnesia blue (#38bdf8), cryo frost, scar red
**Prompt**: A figure in a torn cryo-pod gown stands on the lip of an open airlock at the edge of a ship's hull. Their eyes are white — freshly wiped, no memories behind them yet. Behind them: a starfield opening like a wound. In front of them: one step into the void. One bare foot is already lifted. The Fool's leap, but the cliff is a cargo bay and the valley is the cosmos.
**Mood**: Blank slate about to act. Terrified courage.

## CARD 1 — THE ARCHITECT (Magician)
**Palette**: Crimson (#ef4444), chrome black, surveillance red, glitch white
**Prompt**: A tall figure in a sharp crimson-and-black coat, back half-turned, one hand raised to rearrange floating geometric icons that represent the building blocks of reality — Air, Fire, Water, Earth, Code, Surveillance. His face is a perfectly symmetrical mask of chrome with a single horizontal red slit where eyes should be. Everything in the composition is mirror-symmetric except one small asymmetry he is deliberately ignoring.
**Mood**: Predetermined design. Calm, closed, unkind.

## CARD 2 — THE DREAMER (High Priestess)
**Palette**: Deep indigo (#4338ca), gold (#fbbf24), astral purple, silver veil
**Prompt**: A veiled figure seated between two pillars — one labeled PAST in gold script, one labeled FUTURE in gold script. The veil is translucent; behind it you can see not a face but a star map. Her hands rest on a holographic scroll that is currently displaying a timeline ten seconds from now. A moon-crescent crown floats above the veil without touching it.
**Mood**: Receptive, certain, gently sad.

## CARD 3 — THE EMPRESS ELARA (Empress)
**Palette**: Emerald (#10b981), warm gold, senator cream, circuit green
**Prompt**: A woman in a senator's flowing robe that is subtly rendered as a ship's exterior paneling along its hem and cuffs — human at the torso, Inception Ark at the extremities. She holds a sleeping infant in one arm (the cryogenic population) and a data-tablet scepter in the other. Behind her: the curve of the Ark's observation deck. The look on her face is the look of a mother who has already decided to outlive everyone she loves.
**Mood**: Abundant care. Programmed grief.

## CARD 4 — THE EMPEROR LOCKE (Emperor)
**Palette**: Slate (#64748b), legal brass, gavel mahogany, blue-steel
**Prompt**: Adjudicator Locke seated on a throne that is also a bench — half emperor, half judge. The throne is assembled from stacked law books and crystalline data shards. He wears a military-jurist coat with a high collar. One hand rests on a scale, the other on a gavel. His expression is tired but unwavering. Behind him: the interlocking gears of a functioning constitution.
**Mood**: Structure as a kindness. Tired, incorruptible.

## CARD 5 — THE HIEROPHANT / ANTIQUARIAN (Hierophant)
**Palette**: Aged parchment (#fef3c7), amber (#d97706), temporal blue, spine-gold
**Prompt**: The Antiquarian as an ancient bearded scholar in long layered robes covered in tiny handwritten notations. He is seated at a lectern on which rests an open book; the book's pages are impossibly deep, each page a different timeline. Behind him: twelve closed books on a shelf, each labeled with a Roman numeral — the twelve possible endings he has already catalogued. A student figure kneels before him accepting a scroll.
**Mood**: Forbidden knowledge gently offered.

## CARD 6 — THE TWO WITNESSES (Lovers)
**Palette**: Dual sunset (#f97316 and #6366f1), timeline gold, mirror silver
**Prompt**: Two identical figures of the same person facing each other across a narrow chasm, one in orange-golden light (the timeline that chose one thing) and one in indigo shadow (the timeline that chose the other). They are linked at the wrists by a single glowing cord that is beginning to fray. Above them a scale hangs in mid-collapse, tipping decisively toward one side while the other witness watches it fall.
**Mood**: Branching decision. Two selves learning one has to end.

## CARD 7 — THE IRON LION (Chariot)
**Palette**: Burnished iron (#78716c), fire orange (#f97316), insurgency green, spark white
**Prompt**: The Iron Lion standing in the gun-turret of a wheeled armored crawler mid-charge, one foot on the rail, mane of unkempt hair and a long insurgency coat streaming behind him. Fire and sparks trail the vehicle; the treads throw up broken Architect drones like water. He has the wide exultant grin of somebody running straight at the biggest target on the horizon and winning.
**Mood**: Momentum into contact. Jubilant, unsubtle.

## CARD 8 — THE BALANCE (Justice / Adjustment)
**Palette**: Cold white (#f1f5f9), moral-axis blue, truth gold, correction red
**Prompt**: A blindfolded female figure holding a sword UPWARD and a pair of scales in which the four moral axes (TRUTH / DEFIANCE / EMPATHY / ACCEPTANCE) are rendered as glowing glyphs hovering over the pans. The scales are currently in false balance — three pans level, one pan offset — and the figure's free hand is reaching to NUDGE the offset pan back into true. Her blindfold is the only thing about her that isn't perfectly calibrated.
**Mood**: Quiet correction. Not punishment — adjustment.

## CARD 9 — THE ANTIQUARIAN (Hermit)
**Palette**: Faded violet (#a78bfa), ancient cream, lantern gold, void black
**Prompt**: A hooded hermit standing alone on a small, windswept asteroid at the edge of a galaxy. He holds a lantern — but inside the lantern, instead of a flame, there is a tiny turning six-pointed star representing the I Ching's hexagram engine. His staff is a long twisted branch of dark wood. Behind him: the entire Dischordian cosmology rendered in constellation form. He is looking down at his feet, not out at the stars.
**Mood**: Retreat that has become clarity.

## CARD 10 — THE WHEEL (Fortune)
**Palette**: Roulette red (#dc2626), velvet black, chrome silver, sparks
**Prompt**: An enormous wheel of blackened chrome and polished bone, spinning in the dead center of the card. Four creatures at the cardinal points — a serpent descending, a jackal ascending, a raven watching, and a lion roaring. At the hub of the wheel a single indifferent eye. The wheel is mid-rotation; one of the spokes is subtly broken.
**Mood**: Fate as mechanism. Broken, spinning anyway.

## CARD 11 — THE JAILER (Strength / Lust)
**Palette**: Rust red (#b45309), containment amber, enforcer grey, cell-bar black
**Prompt**: A massively-built armored figure holding the maw of a lion-shaped containment beast shut with both hands — not by force, by patience. He is not fighting the beast. He is waiting it out. The beast's eyes are molten containment-cell red; his are exhausted and kind. Around them, a grid of prison bars that extends up into the sky like a cage with no ceiling.
**Mood**: Endurance as discipline. The function, grieving the role.

## CARD 12 — THE HANGED ONE (Hanged Man)
**Palette**: Oracle white (#f8fafc), processing indigo, suspension gold, signal static
**Prompt**: The White Oracle suspended upside-down inside a holographic processing loop — not a rope, a lattice of running code that hangs her from the ankle. Her hair falls toward the bottom of the card, her eyes are open and calm, and one hand is extended palm-out toward the viewer as if she is still broadcasting. Behind her: the geometric pattern of an audit trail running backward through time.
**Mood**: Voluntary pause becoming revelation. Imprisoned and still reporting.

## CARD 13 — DEATH / THE NECROMANCER (Death)
**Palette**: Ash grey (#57534e), bone white, grave violet, unmaking blue
**Prompt**: The Necromancer as a robed figure walking away from the viewer across a field of soft grey ash that is actually made of quietly dissolving cards. In his wake, behind him, small flowers of bone-white light have begun to grow. He is not a skeleton. He is a person whose face is hidden because if you saw it you would recognize him and he does not want to do that to you.
**Mood**: Transformation that requires an ending. Grief-shaped mercy.

## CARD 14 — THE ENGINEER (Art / Temperance)
**Palette**: Brass (#b45309), moog green, solder silver, blueprint cyan
**Prompt**: The Engineer at a workbench, mid-process, pouring one glowing liquid into another glowing liquid and watching a third color emerge from the merge. He wears his signature goggles (slightly too large, as the Oracle noted) and a leather work apron over a dark jumpsuit. On the bench: a chessboard, a sampler, a mathematics notebook, and a small chess set carved from unfinished wood. Behind him, a floating equation that is resolving itself in real time.
**Mood**: Invention as alchemy. Joyful, recursive.

## CARD 15 — THE COLLECTOR (Devil)
**Palette**: Rose gold (#e11d48), obsidian, collection vitrine glass, chain bronze
**Prompt**: The Collector seated on a throne of display cases full of precious things — a pressed flower, a lover's ring, a child's drawing, an old receipt. He is chained at the ankles by bronze chains of his own forging. His hand is extended toward the viewer palm-up, offering another chain — beautiful and obviously optional. Behind him, shelves of vitrines extend to infinity.
**Mood**: Attachment as bondage. Sorrowful, seductive.

## CARD 16 — THE TOWER (Tower)
**Palette**: Impact orange (#ea580c), ruin black, lightning white, debris grey
**Prompt**: The Panopticon's central reality-tower at the exact moment of the Fall — a lightning bolt from a cloudless sky has struck the apex, and the entire upper third of the spire is detonating outward in slow motion. Two small figures fall from the wreckage with arms outstretched. The lightning bolt is perfectly straight and reads as a decision, not an accident.
**Mood**: The hour a false structure comes down. Grief and clarity in the same frame.

## CARD 17 — THE POTENTIALS (Star)
**Palette**: Cryo blue (#38bdf8), hope silver, starfield indigo, vault-light warm
**Prompt**: A woman in a Senate cloak kneeling at the edge of a cryogenic vault, pouring a jar of liquid starlight into the vault and a second jar of starlight out onto the ground. Above her: one enormous central star with seven smaller stars surrounding it — the traditional Star-card composition. The cryo-tubes in the vault each contain a sleeping face. Every face is slightly smiling.
**Mood**: Hope as engineering. Offered, not earned.

## CARD 18 — THE MOON TYRANT (Moon)
**Palette**: Oppressive silver (#cbd5e1), fear indigo, broadcast red, shadow deep-black
**Prompt**: An enormous orbital surveillance moon hanging over a dark landscape. From its underside it projects two beams of anxious red light that fall on a wolf and a dog howling at it from the ground, and between them a small crayfish (RAW homage) crawling out of a pool. The moon has a human face on its surface, eyes half-closed in deliberate neutrality, broadcasting a field the creatures on the ground cannot see but feel on every frequency.
**Mood**: Illusion as weapon. Named and still effective.

## CARD 19 — THE SOURCE (Sun)
**Palette**: Bright gold (#facc15), mercy cream, plague green undertone, ascension white
**Prompt**: A smiling androgynous figure haloed in brilliant golden light, arms open in a welcoming gesture. Two small children dance in front of them in what first reads as joy and on second glance reads as compulsion. Behind the figure: a field of sunflowers that are all turning toward it rather than the sky. One sunflower in the foreground has been gently broken at the stem and is still smiling anyway.
**Mood**: Generosity that will consume you. Warm, unsurvivable.

## CARD 20 — JUDGMENT / THE VOTE (Judgment / Aeon)
**Palette**: Trumpet gold (#eab308), vote-red, vote-blue, ballot white, resurrection flame
**Prompt**: A winged figure — neither angelic nor demonic, just civic — blowing a long brass trumpet from a high vantage. Below, a crowd of people (living, sleeping, and newly-reanimated) rising out of coffin-shaped voting booths with paper ballots held high. The trumpet blast is rendered as a readable ballot in mid-air that everyone can see. The figure's wings have ballot-style perforations along their edges.
**Mood**: Public decision that matters more than the voters know.

## CARD 21 — THE UNIVERSE (World)
**Palette**: Cosmic violet (#7c3aed), life green, infinity gold, universal black
**Prompt**: A dancing female figure at the exact center of an ouroboros-framed cosmos. Behind her: a Dischordian galactic map, every Ark, every inhabited system, every named NPC as a small constellation. She holds two wands (or two sampler sticks, Engineer's touch) and is mid-spin. The ouroboros around her is eating its own tail with obvious relief.
**Mood**: Completion that remains in motion. The whole thing acknowledging itself.

## CARD 22 — THE FNORD (no traditional equivalent)
**Palette**: Fnord rose (#f43f5e), glitch cyan, RAW yellow, 23-enigma black
**Prompt**: A card that is deliberately slightly wrong. The frame is askew by 1 degree. The numeral in the top corner reads "23" in the same Art Deco style as every other card, but no other card in the deck is numbered 23. The central image is a half-drawn figure rendered in the same painterly style as the others but NEVER RESOLVING into anything recognizable — suggestions of a face, a hand, a sampler pad, a playing card, a door, all of them crossfaded. A single perfectly-drawn yellow "23" glyph floats near the center with no explanation. Robert Anton Wilson cameo reference: if you look long enough at the background you see the word FNORD repeated in faint lettering, but only when you're not looking directly at it.
**Mood**: The card the deck pretends it does not have. The card that is always doing something to the reading.

## USAGE NOTES

- Every prompt above assumes the Nano Banana style anchor suffix
  from the preamble — append it when sending to the pipeline.
- Cards 0-21 are the "honest" 22-card tarot shape in Dischordian
  drag. The Fnord (22) is the deck's RAW homage and must be
  generated last so it can be subtly inconsistent with the other
  22 without that inconsistency being the point of the card.
- Palettes are suggestive; if Nano Banana renders a better read
  in a related hue, follow the better read.
- Numeral (0-22) should always be visible in the top-center
  cartouche. This is the single UI requirement that outranks
  painterly concerns.
- Card names are rendered in the frame, not in the art. Do NOT
  ask Nano Banana to letter the name into the painting — the
  TCG frame supplies it.
