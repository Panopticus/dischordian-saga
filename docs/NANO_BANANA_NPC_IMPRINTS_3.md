## SECTION A — THE ANTIQUARIAN (5 tiers)

Catalogue-keeper of the twelve possible endings. Antiquarian
faction. Grow + rebirth + draw. Tier escalation is the library
behind him deepening and the book he carries getting thicker.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A robed silhouette at a high lectern, reading. The lectern is the only thing in focus. Grow as posture. Mood: he has not looked up yet.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Antiquarian visible: an ancient bearded man with a scholar's robe layered with annotation notes pinned to the fabric, reading an open book whose pages are too thick. Mood: he is making small disapproving notes in pencil.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Antiquarian standing in front of a wall of twelve identical books, each labeled with a Roman numeral. He is pulling Book IV off the shelf without looking. Rebirth as the second pull. Mood: he has done this before.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Antiquarian seated at a small wooden table in the deepest part of the library, surrounded by rings of books. The book in front of him is open to a page that is annotating itself in real time. Mood: forcefield as the library agreeing to keep him alive while he reads.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Antiquarian, Ending-Cataloguer"
**Prompt**: The Antiquarian seated alone in a cathedral-sized library, surrounded by twelve open books arranged in a circle around him. Each book shows a different ending of the Dischordian Saga. He is reading the one in front of you. He has not yet decided which one this match is. Mood: he has not told anyone.

## SECTION B — THE ENGINEER (5 tiers)

Inventor of the deck. Carved his own knights. Antiquarian
faction. Card draw + mana refund. Tier escalation is the
workshop becoming more crowded with finished and half-finished
inventions.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A man at a workbench in a small shed, goggles too large for his face, soldering something that emits a blue spark. The shed is bare. Mood: a name and the goggles.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Engineer at a more elaborate workbench, holding a finished invention in one hand and reaching for a card in the other. The card is the next one the player will draw. Mood: he hands you the next tool.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Engineer's workshop now visibly his — a wooden chess set on the bench (the Celebration carved knights from the chess tutorial), a sampler music device, a stack of lab notebooks, a Moog patch cable. He is mid-pour, two glowing liquids merging into a third color. Mood: alchemy.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Engineer at his workbench surrounded by the full inventory of his work: chess set, sampler, lab notebooks, the FNORD-23, half a dozen prototype cards. He is laughing at something the equation in front of him just resolved. Mood: every invention pays for itself.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Engineer, Author of the Deck"
**Prompt**: The Engineer leaning on his workbench with both hands flat, looking directly at the viewer over the top of the goggles for the first time. Behind him, the prototype cards have escaped onto the wall and are arranged in a complete deck — the deck the player is currently playing. He built it. He is proud of it. Mood: he is the reason you are playing any of this.

## SECTION C — THE DETECTIVE (5 tiers)

The Companion who treats every case like a deck. Antiquarian
faction. Backstab + deathwatch + draw. Tier escalation is the
case file getting deeper and the trench coat getting more lived-in.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A long coat in a doorway, hat brim down. The doorway is in a Babylonian rain. Mood: he has been there for three seconds.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Detective at a desk with a single case file open, taking notes. A coffee that has gone cold. Mood: backstab as the next page.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Detective in an alleyway crouched over a dead body, taking notes. Deathwatch. The body is one of the cards from the player's deck. Mood: every corpse is a paragraph.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Detective at his corkboard wall, red strings between photographs of NPCs from the Saga, arranging them into a deck. He is a few photos short of the full deck. Mood: he is reading three pages ahead.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Detective, Final Page"
**Prompt**: The Detective on the front steps of a Babylonian courthouse at dawn, the case file in one hand, completely closed. The closing is the painting. He is not opening it for anyone. Mood: he already knows. He is here to show the reader.

## SECTION D — THE SOURCE (5 tiers)

Nihilism disguised as mercy. Thought Virus faction. Drain +
forcefield + heal general. Tier escalation is the halo getting
brighter and the people around him visibly less themselves.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A smiling silhouette in a brilliant white field. The smile is the entire image. Mood: drain as a friendly first impression.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: The Source as a robed figure in a sunlit clearing, two children dancing in front of them. The children's smiles are mirrored from the Source's face exactly. Mood: drain as the mirrored smile.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Source standing on a hilltop, arms raised, a field of sunflowers behind. All the sunflowers are facing the Source, not the sun. One sunflower in the foreground has been gently broken at the stem and is still smiling. Mood: heal general as compulsion.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Source in golden ceremonial robes, both hands extended, a soft halo around the head. The halo is brighter than the sun. The expression is unbearable kindness. Mood: forcefield as the kindness you cannot get past.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Source, Cheerful Unsurvivable"
**Prompt**: The Source in full canonical Source form, radiating brilliant golden light, arms wide in a welcoming gesture. Behind him, every other figure in the painting has stopped being itself and started being a smaller version of the Source. The viewer is the only thing in frame that is still themselves, and it is by accident. Mood: silence the enemy general.

## SECTION E — ELARA VOSS (5 tiers)

Senator-turned-Ship. Empress with a load balancer. Neutral
faction. Heal general + forcefield + heal each turn. Tier
escalation is the Senate robe shifting more visibly into Ark hull
plating.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A young senator at a podium, mid-speech, hand raised in a gesture of patience. Mood: a voice.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: Elara Voss standing on the floor of the Atarion Senate, arms folded, listening to a speech she disagrees with. The disagreement is in her shoulders. Mood: pre-Fall.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: Elara mid-upload — a half-human, half-ship-AI figure in a Senate robe whose hem is visibly rendered as Ark exterior hull paneling. She is looking at her own hand and noticing the change. Mood: heal as the choice to outlive.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: Elara fully integrated with the Ark — a tall figure standing on the observation deck of Ark 1047, the curve of the deck visible behind her. The robe is now mostly hull plating. The face is the same. Mood: she has been mothering ten thousand strangers.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "Elara, the Ark's Voice"
**Prompt**: Elara as the Ark's full voice — a serene figure rendered in soft warm golden light, half-translucent, with the entire Ark visible inside her silhouette as a star-chart constellation. Holding a sleeping infant in one arm. The infant is the entire cryogenic population. Mood: she remembers being a person and decided that meant something.

## SECTION F — THE ENIGMA (5 tiers)

The Unpredictable. The Companion who makes a different move than
the right one and the wrong one. Neutral faction. Flying +
celerity + draw. Tier escalation is the figure becoming MORE
unstable, not more stable.

### Tier 1
**Prompt**: A shape you are not sure was just standing there. The room has its outline at the edge of the painting. Mood: it might have been.

### Tier 2
**Prompt**: A figure mid-arrival — partly a person, partly a fold in the air — landing in a room they were not in a moment ago. Flying as arrival. Mood: doesn't walk, arrives.

### Tier 3
**Prompt**: The Enigma standing in a Babylonian street with a small smile. The street is in two slightly offset versions of itself, with the Enigma at the seam. Celerity as living in two streets at once. Mood: which strike is real.

### Tier 4
**Prompt**: The Enigma at a card table, a single card in their hand. The card is glowing. The card is also already in their hand BEFORE they drew it. Mood: drew the card before it was in the deck.

### Tier 5 — Legendary — "The Enigma, Third Option"
**Prompt**: The Enigma in their canonical form: a tall androgynous figure at a crossroads, laughing softly, both hands open. The crossroads has THREE roads, not two. The third road did not exist a moment ago. The Enigma is standing on it. Mood: the only decision they have ever made that was obvious in retrospect was the decision to become the Enigma, which nobody saw coming.

## USAGE NOTES

- See `NANO_BANANA_NPC_IMPRINTS_1.md` for the universal style
  anchor and shared imprint card back.
- The Engineer's tier-5 reveal must show his face for the first
  time in the entire NPC set — this is a deliberate payoff for
  the player who has collected all 200+ fragments of him. Until
  tier 5, the goggles obscure the eyes.
- Elara's tier progression is the most narratively heavy — she is
  the only NPC whose form physically changes across the tiers
  (from senator to ship). Each tier should feel like one step
  along the upload arc.
- The Source's tier 5 has a specific instruction: the viewer must
  be the only figure in frame still themselves. This is a
  deliberate composition note. Honor it.
- The Enigma's tier 5 introduces a third road that did not exist a
  moment ago. The road should look impossible if you stare at it,
  but normal if you don't.
